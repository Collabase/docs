Test Management keeps what you test and what came out of it. Manual testers work through cases in Collabase. Your CI pipeline sends its results in, and both end up in the same cycle.

## Structure

```text
Test project (key prefix, for example QA)
├── Folders (any depth)
│   └── Test cases (QA-T12)
│       └── Versions (draft, in review, approved, superseded)
│           └── Steps
└── Cycles (QA-C3)
    └── Executions (one per case in the cycle)
        └── Results (every entry is kept, a correction is a new entry)
```

Keys are stable. `QA-T12` is the twelfth case of the project with the prefix `QA`, and it stays `QA-T12` when you move or rename it. Addresses use the key too, for example `/my-space/test-management/QA/cases/QA-T12`.

## A typical flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write a case">
    Create it in a folder with steps and expected results. It starts as a draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get it approved">
    Submit the version for review. A reviewer approves it, or sends it back with a reason.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan a cycle">
    Pick cases, folders or all approved cases, and assign them to testers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run and import">
    Testers record results step by step. CI results arrive through the import.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check coverage">
    Open the reports to see which requirements have no passing test, and close the cycle when the release is done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Space administrator** | Create projects, configure rules and roles, archive and delete |
| **Test manager** | Write and organize cases, plan cycles, assign tests |
| **Tester** | Run assigned tests, add files and raise work items |
| **Reviewer** | Approve or reject case versions |
| **Reader** | See cases, cycles and results |

A project is open to everyone in the space until you name people or groups in its roles. From then on only those people and space administrators can open it.
