In the traditional approach, Deployment ensures a safe transition to the new state. Operation is launched.

## What happens in Deployment

- Deployment measures such as user training are carried out.
- Depending on the scenario, the migration is carried out.
- The product or system and the organization are activated.
- Operation is activated.
- The information security and data protection concept is handed over.
- During the first period of operation, until acceptance, the project supports problem analysis and resolution. After that, the warranty and regular operation begin.

## How Deployment ends

The **closure phase release** decision is taken and the resources for Closure are released. After a successful launch of operation, the **acceptance** decision is taken and the phase ends.

## In Collabase

- Plan the deployment measures as tasks with dates, so they show on the [Roadmap](/projects/roadmap).
- Collect problems from the first period of operation in a [queue](/projects/queues), so they are triaged in one place.
- Record the acceptance in an **Acceptance report** page in [Collabase Docs](/docs/pages), created from a [template](/docs/templates).
