HERMES is the project management method of the Swiss Federal Administration. Federal offices, cantons, municipalities and many companies close to the public sector use it to run IT, organisation and service projects in a structured, comparable way.

This guide explains the elements of HERMES 2022 in short, practical chapters. Every chapter ends with a section on how to map that element in Collabase, using Projects, Collabase Docs, Registry and Automation.

<Note>
  HERMES is a method of the Swiss Federal Chancellery. Collabase is not affiliated with the Federal Chancellery, and this guide is not an official HERMES publication. For binding definitions, use the official reference manual on [HERMES online](https://www.hermes.admin.ch/en/).
</Note>
