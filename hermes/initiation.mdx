Initiation is the first phase of every HERMES project, traditional or agile. It creates a defined starting point: the goals are aligned with the requirements of the core organization, the possible solutions are known, and the way forward is decided.

## What happens in Initiation

- The project sponsor releases the resources for Initiation, based on the **project initiation order**, and assigns the project manager.
- The **study** is prepared: a first situation assessment, goals and rough requirements, the solution variants, and the project and operating risks.
- The **legal basis analysis** and the **protection needs analysis** are prepared alongside the study.
- For each variant, the team documents whether the solution will be developed traditionally or agile. The **next steps** decision is taken.
- If a product or system has to be procured, a **procurement analysis** runs in parallel. HERMES plans procurement as early as Initiation.
- The matching **scenario** is selected and adapted if needed.
- The **project management plan** and the **execution order** are prepared and aligned with the strategies and goals of the core organization. Stakeholder interests are analysed.
- In the agile approach, the team decides whether release milestones are mandatory.

## How Initiation ends

At the end of Initiation, the core organization and the project sponsor decide whether the project continues. If it does, the **execution release** decision is taken and the execution order is signed. Reasons to stop include poor cost-effectiveness, excessive risk, lack of feasibility, legal or political concerns, or a mismatch with the goals of the organization.

## Key outcomes

| Outcome | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Project initiation order** | Basis for releasing the resources for Initiation |
| **Study** | Situation, goals, requirements, solution variants and risks |
| **Legal basis analysis** | Legal requirements for the solution |
| **Protection needs analysis** | Information security and data protection needs |
| **Procurement analysis** | Whether and how to procure |
| **Project management plan** | How the project is planned and controlled |
| **Execution order** | The signed mandate for the development of the solution |

## In Collabase

- Write the study, the project management plan and the execution order in [Collabase Docs](/docs/pages), each from a [template](/docs/templates). Version history keeps every state of a document traceable.
- Record the solution variants and the next steps decision in a **Decision** object type in [Registry](/registry/overview).
- Record the risks identified in the study in a **Risk** object type in the same Registry schema.
- Model the next steps and execution release milestones as tasks and route them through an approval. See [Milestones and decisions](/hermes/milestones).
