Closure is the last phase of every HERMES project, traditional or agile. It provides a structure for winding the project down in an orderly way.

## What happens in Closure

- The outcomes are checked for completeness and completed from a formal point of view.
- The **final project evaluation** is reviewed and approved where needed.
- The outcomes and documentation are handed over to the core organization, for the application, operating and maintenance organization.
- The documentation of the project is archived according to the filing rules of the core organization.
- Depending on the scenario, the legacy system is taken out of operation and removed, together with infrastructure no longer needed. Old data is archived or destroyed.
- The project organization is dissolved. Before that, the team checks whether parts of it can move to the application organization.

## How Closure ends

The **project closure** decision is taken. Open items are handed over to the core organization and the application organization, and the project is complete.

## In Collabase

- Write the final project evaluation and the lessons learned in [Collabase Docs](/docs/pages).
- Move open items to a project of the application organization. A work item can move to another project, including one in another Space. See [Tasks](/projects/tasks).
- Once the project is closed, the project owner can archive it.
