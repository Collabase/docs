It covers both operating models: Collabase Cloud, which we run for you in Swiss data centers, and self-hosted Collabase on your own infrastructure.

## Report a vulnerability

Send security findings to [support@collabase.ch](mailto:support@collabase.ch). The contact is also published in machine-readable form at [collabase.ch/.well-known/security.txt](https://collabase.ch/.well-known/security.txt) (RFC 9116). We read reports in German, English, French and Italian.
