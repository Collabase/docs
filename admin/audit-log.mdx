The audit log records changes and security events across the whole installation: who signed in, who changed a permission, who deleted a page, which agent changed a work item. Use it to answer "who did this, and when?" after an incident, and as evidence for an audit.

<WhoCanDoThis role="System administrator" edition="Collabase Enterprise Edition" />

## Open the audit log

Go to **Administration → Audit Log**. The newest entries come first, 50 at a time. Click **Load more** at the bottom for older ones.

Nobody can edit or delete an entry from this screen. Entries are written by Collabase itself when something happens.

---

## What an entry shows {#what-an-entry-shows}

| Column | Details |
|---|---|
| **Timestamp** | Date and time to the second |
| **Actor** | The person who acted, with IP address, operating system and browser. **System** when Collabase acted on its own, for example when it cleans up the trash. |
| **Action** | What happened, for example **Signed in**, **User locked** or `PAGE_DELETED` |
| **Entity** | The kind of thing affected, for example `Page`, `User` or `Space`, with its name or ID below |
| **Summary** | One sentence that describes the change |

Click a row to open its details:

| Section | Details |
|---|---|
| **Context Details** | **Actor**, **Email**, **IP Address**, **Environment** (operating system and browser), **Timestamp** and **Entity ID** |
| **Data Changes** | The values **Before** and **After** the change, side by side, when the entry records a change |
| **Raw Details** | Everything the entry stores, when it records an event rather than a change |

---

## Find an entry {#find-an-entry}

Two filters sit above the list. They combine, so you can show, for example, only deletions of pages.

| Filter | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Entity** | **All Entities**, or one of `Page`, `Space`, `User`, `Group`, `Task`, `Project`, `Meeting`, `CustomField`, `Automation`, `App`, `ApiToken`, `PdfTemplate`, `PdfExport`, `Trash`, `Session`, `System` |
| **Action** | **All Actions**, or one action, such as `USER_GLOBAL_ROLE_CHANGED` or `SECURITY_POLICY_CHANGED` |

### Questions and where to look

| Question | Filter |
|---|---|
| Who made this person an administrator? | **Action** `USER_GLOBAL_ROLE_CHANGED` |
| Who changed a password or session rule? | **Action** `SECURITY_POLICY_CHANGED` |
| Who deleted this page? | **Entity** `Page`, **Action** `PAGE_DELETED` |
| What did the identity provider change? | **Action** `SCIM_USER_PROVISIONED`, `SCIM_USER_UPDATED`, `SCIM_USER_DEPROVISIONED` or `SCIM_USER_REJECTED` |
| When did someone sign in, and from where? | **Action** `AUTH_SIGNED_IN` or `AUTH_SIGN_IN_FAILED` |
| What did an AI agent change? | **Action** `MCP_TOOL_WRITE` |
| Who emptied or restored something in the trash? | **Entity** `Trash` |

## What is recorded {#what-is-recorded}

| Area | Examples |
|---|---|
| Sign-in and account security | Signed in, sign-in failed, signed out, session ended, password changed or reset, second factor enabled or reset, passkey added, account locked and unlocked |
| Users and groups | Users created, changed, locked, released, anonymized; instance role changed; group members added or removed; module access changed |
| Identity and provisioning | Identity provider, SCIM token and LDAP settings changed; accounts provisioned, updated or turned away by SCIM; identities linked |
| Security settings | Security policies changed, licence updated or removed |
| Guests | Guests invited or removed, their rights changed, pages shared with guests |
| Content | Spaces, pages, work items, custom fields and automations created, changed or deleted; pages published or restored |
| Trash | Items restored, deleted for good, and cleaned up after their retention |
| Apps and agents | Apps installed or removed, OAuth applications registered, app access granted or withdrawn, changes made by an AI agent |
| Meetings | Meetings cancelled or deleted, participants and organizers changed, notes published |
| Exports | PDF templates changed, PDFs exported, export history cleared |

---

## How long entries are kept {#retention}

| Entries | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Signed in, sign-in failed, signed out | 180 days. A maintenance job that runs every five minutes removes older ones. |
| Every other entry | As long as the installation exists |

The retention is fixed and cannot be changed on the screen. Entries are part of the database, so they are included in every [backup](/admin/backup).

<Note>
  The audit log stores the IP address and the browser of the person who acted. Take this into account in your record of processing activities.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | Cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| The page is blurred and asks for an upgrade | The installation has no Enterprise licence. | Add the licence key under **Administration → License**, see [Licence and editions](/admin/editions-and-license). |
| **No logs found.** | No entry matches both filters. | Set one filter back to **All Entities** or **All Actions**. |
| **The audit log could not be loaded.** | The server could not read the entries. | Reload the page. If it persists, check the application log, see [Troubleshooting](/admin/troubleshooting). |
| **Unknown IP** for every entry | The reverse proxy in front of Collabase does not pass on the visitor's address. | Configure the proxy to send the header `X-Real-IP` or `X-Forwarded-For`. The standard installation does this. |
| An action shows as a code, such as `PAGE_DELETED`, instead of a phrase | Only newer actions have a readable name. | The code names the action. The **Summary** column describes it in words. |
