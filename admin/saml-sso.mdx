Security Assertion Markup Language (SAML) is an XML-based standard that lets an identity provider tell Collabase who has signed in. Members sign in once at your identity provider and reach Collabase without a second password.

Collabase supports SAML 2.0 single sign-on (SSO) that starts at Collabase. A member clicks **Sign in with SSO (SAML)** on the login page, signs in at your identity provider and returns to Collabase signed in.

**Who can do this?**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | System administrator |
| **Edition** | Collabase Enterprise Edition |

SAML only handles authentication. It does not decide what a member may see. Give access through groups and space permissions, see [Permissions](/concepts/permissions). A new account starts with the default role you choose in the SAML settings.

## Supported identity providers

Collabase works with identity providers that implement the SAML 2.0 Web Browser SSO profile, for example Microsoft Entra ID and Okta. Collabase sends the sign-in request to your provider with an HTTP redirect and receives the response at its ACS URL.

For a step-by-step setup with Microsoft, see [IAM with Microsoft Entra ID](/admin/iam-microsoft-entra).

## Before you begin

- Have an active Enterprise licence. Identity providers are part of Collabase Enterprise Edition.
- Reach Collabase over HTTPS, and make sure the base URL Collabase is configured with is the HTTPS one.
- Keep one administrator who signs in with email and password. If your identity provider is unreachable or misconfigured, this account gets you back in.
- Decide which attribute of your identity provider is the email address of a member, and use the same one for SCIM if you provision accounts.

## How sign-in works

- The member opens the Collabase login page and clicks **Sign in with SSO (SAML)**.
- Collabase sends the member to the single sign-on URL of your identity provider.
- The identity provider signs the member in and sends a signed response to the ACS URL of Collabase.
- Collabase checks the signature, the audience, the validity period and that the response answers a sign-in it started. Then it finds the account.

Sign-in always starts at Collabase. A sign-in that starts in the portal of your identity provider, for example from a tile in My Apps, is refused.

## Set up your identity provider

The exact process varies by provider. You usually need to:

- Define an application in your identity provider.
- Give it the values from the table below.
- Send the two attributes from the second table with the sign-in.
- Sign the SAML assertion.
- Assign the people who may sign in.

Open **Admin → Identity Providers**, add **SAML 2.0** and keep the **Configure SAML 2.0** panel open. Under **Enter these values at your identity provider** Collabase lists what your provider needs:

| Value | Format |
|---|---|
| **Assertion consumer service (ACS) URL** | `https://your-collabase-domain/api/auth/sso/saml2/sp/acs/saml` |
| **Service provider entity ID** (also called audience) | `https://your-collabase-domain/api/auth/sso/saml2/sp/metadata?providerId=saml`. This is the metadata URL. You cannot change it. |
| **Service provider metadata URL** | `https://your-collabase-domain/api/auth/sso/saml2/sp/metadata?providerId=saml` |

Most providers can import the whole configuration from the metadata URL. The audience of the response must be the entity ID above, or Collabase refuses it.

### Attributes Collabase reads

| Attribute | What Collabase does with it |
|---|---|
| `email` | The address Collabase uses to find the account. Without this attribute, Collabase uses the name identifier of the response as the address. |
| `displayName` | The name of the member for a new account. Without it, Collabase uses the name identifier. |
| `givenName` and `surname` | Optional. When your provider sends them under these exact names, together they form the name and take precedence over `displayName`. |

Collabase reads the attributes by these exact names. Providers that send claims under long names, such as Microsoft Entra ID by default, need an additional claim with the plain name.

<Warning>
  Collabase refuses a response whose assertion is not signed. Set your provider to sign the assertion, or the assertion and the response.
</Warning>

## Configure SAML in Collabase

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the SAML settings">
    Open **Admin → Identity Providers**, add **SAML 2.0** and open **Configure SAML 2.0**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the values of your identity provider">
    Enter the values from the table below, or paste the metadata address of your provider under **Import from metadata URL** and click **Import**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the behavior">
    Set **Default Role for new users** and **Auto-create new users**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable and save">
    Switch on **Enable provider** and click **Save**. The login page now shows **Sign in with SSO (SAML)**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="The Configure SAML 2.0 panel. The values at the bottom are the ones your identity provider needs.">
  <img src="/images/iam-saml-settings.png" alt="The Configure SAML 2.0 panel with the import field, the three provider values, the behavior switches and the values for your identity provider" width="420" />
</Frame>

| Setting | Details |
|---|---|
| **Import from metadata URL** | Reads the metadata of your identity provider and fills in the three fields below. Nothing is saved until you click **Save**. Only public HTTPS addresses work. |
| **Identity provider entity ID** | The identifier of your provider, sometimes called issuer or Entity ID. Collabase accepts sign-ins only from this provider, which ties sign-in to your organization. |
| **Single sign-on URL** | The address your provider listens on. Collabase sends members there to sign in. |
| **Signing certificate** | The public certificate of your provider in PEM format, starting with `-----BEGIN CERTIFICATE-----`. Collabase checks every signature with it. Paste a new certificate to replace the stored one, or leave the field as it is to keep it. |
| **Default Role for new users** | The role of an account that Collabase creates at the first sign-in: **User** or **Admin**. Keep **User**. |
| **Auto-create new users** | On: Collabase creates an account at the first sign-in when none exists. Off: the account must exist beforehand, for example through SCIM or because an administrator created it. |
| **Enable provider** | Shows the sign-in button on the login page. |

After you save, the expanded SAML row shows the identity provider entity ID, the single sign-on URL, the ACS URL and the service provider entity ID.

## First sign-in and account matching

Collabase matches a sign-in to an account by **email address**. At the first sign-in it decides like this:

| Situation | Result |
|---|---|
| Exactly one active account has the email address | Collabase links it to the identity. Later sign-ins are recognized by the identity of the provider. |
| No account has the address and **Auto-create new users** is on | Collabase creates an account with the default role, if a licensed seat is free. |
| No account has the address and **Auto-create new users** is off | The sign-in is refused. |
| More than one account has the address | The sign-in is refused. |
| The account is deactivated | The sign-in is refused. |
| The account is a guest | The sign-in is refused. A guest cannot be linked to an identity provider. |

When SCIM provisions your accounts, switch **Auto-create new users** off and send the same email address through SCIM and at sign-in. A mismatch then stops with a clear message instead of creating a second account.

## Best practices

- Use HTTPS on both sides, for Collabase and for your identity provider.
- Keep the clocks in sync. A SAML response is valid for a short time. Collabase accepts a clock difference of five minutes between your server and your provider. Synchronize both with a time server.
- Note when the signing certificate of your provider expires. When it issues a new one, paste both certificates into **Signing certificate** until the old one has expired, so sign-in keeps working during the change.
- Send your members to the Collabase login page, not to the portal of your identity provider.
- Keep one local administrator. The email and password login stays available at `/auth/login`.

## Troubleshooting

| What the member sees | Cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| This sign-in could not be matched to a Collabase account. | No single active account has the email address of the sign-in, **Auto-create new users** is off, the account is a guest, or two accounts share the address. | Compare the address in Collabase with the one your provider sends. Make them identical. |
| The response from your identity provider was not accepted. | The assertion is not signed, the audience differs from the service provider entity ID, the response is outside its validity period, or the sign-in did not start at Collabase. | Check the signing option, the identifier in your provider, the clocks, and that the member started at the Collabase login page. |
| Your account has been disabled. | The account is deactivated, by SCIM or by an administrator. | Release the account in **Admin → Users**, or assign the person again in your provider. |
| No licence seat is free for a new account. | All licensed seats are in use. | Free a seat or extend the licence. |
| The member lands on an error page of your provider. | The problem is on the provider side, for example a person who is not assigned to the application. | Use the support and tools of your identity provider. |
