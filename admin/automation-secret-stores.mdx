With a secret store, a connection holds only a reference. Collabase reads the secret just before a step runs and does not keep it. Manage stores under **Administration, Secret stores**.

## Connect a store

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the provider">
    Select **Connect a store** and choose **HashiCorp Vault** or **Azure Key Vault**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    For HashiCorp Vault enter the address, the secrets engine, an optional namespace and a token, or an AppRole role ID and secret ID. For Azure Key Vault enter the vault address, tenant ID, client ID and client secret. Credentials are stored encrypted and never shown again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow a store in your own network">
    Switch on **The store is in our own network** when the store has a private address. Loopback and cloud metadata addresses stay blocked in any case.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Release a store to workspaces

A store is usable only in the workspaces you release it to. Select **Release** on the store and choose the workspaces. Connections of other workspaces cannot point to it.

## Remove a store

Removing a store works only while no connection points to it. The page says when connections still do.
