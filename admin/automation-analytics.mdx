The analytics page shows how much Studio is used and where it fails. System administrators see every workspace. Admins of a workspace see their own workspace under its **Analytics** tab.

## Open it

Open **Administration, Studio analytics**. The page covers all workspaces you administer. Choose the **Period**. The default is the last 30 days.

## What it shows

| Figure | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Active flows** | Flows that are switched on. |
| **Runs per day** | Successful and failed runs for each day, as a chart and as a table (**Show as table**). |
| **Average duration** | How long a run takes from start to finish. |
| **Most used plugs** | The ten plugs that steps use most. |
| **Who builds** | The ten people who saved or published the most, with their counts. |
| **Agent steps and AI tokens** | How often agents worked and how many tokens the AI steps used. |

The figures are collected every hour from the finished runs of the last hours, so they are up to an hour old. A run that ends late is still counted.

## As a dashboard gadget

The same figures are available as the **Studio analytics** gadget on a dashboard. People only see the workspaces they may see.

## Use it

| You see | Do this |
|---|---|
| Failures rise on one day | Open the **Runs** tab, filter by **Failed** and that day, and read the failed step. See [Debugging runs](/automation/debugging-runs). |
| A plug is used by one flow only | Check whether it is worth keeping installed. See [Studio plugs](/admin/automation-plugs). |
| AI tokens grow | Look at the agent steps of the workspace and consider a monthly budget. See [AI provider](/automation/ai-provider). |
