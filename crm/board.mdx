The board shows the records of one type as cards in columns, one column per stage. For opportunities the stages run from **New** to **Won** or **Lost**.

## Switch to the board

Open **Opportunities** and click the board icon next to the column picker. Click the table icon to go back. The board uses the filter bar and the open view, so you can narrow it to your own deals. Save the board as a view to open it that way next time.

Another type shows a board too when it has a choice field. If there is more than one, pick the field to group by next to the switch.

## Read a column

The head of a column shows the stage, the number of deals and the sum of their amounts. Deals in different currencies get one line each, because a sum across currencies would mean nothing. **Won** and **Lost** carry a word next to their colour, so they are clear without colour too.

A column shows the first 50 deals, ordered by close date, with the soonest first and deals without a date last. Click **Load more** at the bottom for the next 50. The numbers in the head always count every deal, not only those you have loaded.

## Move a deal

Drag a card to another column to change its stage. You can also focus a card with the Tab key, press Space to pick it up, move it with the arrow keys and press Space or Enter to drop it. Escape cancels. Any stage can be reached from any other, so a closed deal can be opened again by moving it back.

You need the right to change opportunities and to write the stage field. Without it the card cannot be picked up. If the move cannot be saved, the card jumps back and a message tells you why. When someone else changed the same deal at the same moment, only the stage is written and their other changes stay.

## Say why a deal was lost

Moving a deal to **Lost** opens a window asking for the reason. An administrator can make the reason required under **CRM settings**, then the window cannot be confirmed empty.

## Who is told

When a deal reaches **Won** or **Lost**, its owner and the person who created it get a message, except the person who moved it. People whose role does not allow them to read opportunities get nothing. The message says only that the deal was won or lost, never the amount.

## The board stays current

An open board checks for changes every ten seconds, and about once a minute while its tab is in the background. When a colleague moves a deal, your board follows without a reload.
