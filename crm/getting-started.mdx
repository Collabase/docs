This page takes you from an installation without the CRM to a first company with a note and a task. The first part is for administrators. The second part is for everyone who works in the CRM.

## Switch the CRM on {#switch-on}

The CRM is an experimental app, so it is off until someone switches it on. Choose one of the two ways:

| Who | How | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **A person, for themselves** | Open **Profile → User settings → Collabase Lab** and switch **CRM** on | The app appears for this person only |
| **An administrator, for everyone** | Set `FEATURE_FLAG_OVERRIDES="CRM=true"` in the `.env` file of your installation and restart the app. See [Features in development](/admin/hosting#features-in-development) | The app is on for everyone, and available to all groups that hold it |

A person only sees the app when a group of theirs holds it. See [Who can use it](/crm/overview#who).

## Give the app to groups {#groups}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the group">
    Go to **Administration → Groups**, open a group and choose **App access**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the role">
    Set **CRM** to **User** for people who work in it, or **Admin** for people who manage its model, roles and settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The app now appears in the app drawer of every member of the group. A person without the app does not see it, and its address answers "not found".

## Open the CRM as the first administrator {#first-admin}

The person who first opens the CRM as system administrator becomes **CRM admin**. Open the app once with that account before you give it to others. Then open **CRM** > **Settings** in the system administration and check [roles and settings](/crm/roles):

- Add the people who should work with sales data to the role **Sales**. Add colleagues who only look things up to **Readers**.
- Leave **Contacts from mail** at **None** until you have decided how mail may create contacts. See [Connect your mailbox](/crm/mailbox).

A person without a CRM role sees nothing, even when the app is available to them. Every other role starts empty.

## Create your first company and person {#first-record}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the company">
    Open **Companies** in the sidebar, click **New**, enter the company name and the domain in the dialog **New company**, and save. The CRM looks up the logo of the domain in the background. See [The record page](/crm/record-detail#logo).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the person">
    Open **People**, click **New**, enter first name, last name and an email address in the dialog **New person**, and save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Relate them">
    Open the person, go to **Relations** and add the company. The person appears on the page of the company, and the company shows its people.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You see the new records at once in their lists, with the main domain and the owner as columns. More about lists, filters and columns is on [Companies and people](/crm/objects).

## Add a note and a task {#note-and-task}

On the page of the company:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write a note">
    Open **Notes**, click **New note**, write the text and choose who may read it. See [Notes](/crm/notes).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a task">
    Open **Tasks**, click **New task**, give it a title, a due date and a colleague. The colleague gets a notice, and on the due day a second one. See [CRM tasks](/crm/tasks).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Both entries appear on the **Timeline** of the company next to every change. See [Timeline](/crm/timeline).

## Troubleshooting {#troubleshooting}

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The app is missing from the app drawer | Your group does not hold the CRM, or it is not switched on. Ask an administrator, or switch it on for yourself in **Collabase Lab**. |
| The app opens and shows nothing | You have no CRM role. Ask a CRM admin to add you to a role. |
| A list you expect is missing from the sidebar | Your roles do not let you read that type. See [Why does someone not see a record?](/crm/roles) |
