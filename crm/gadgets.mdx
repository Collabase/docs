The CRM brings five ready gadgets to the gadget catalog. Add them to a dashboard or to your home page like any other gadget.

## Add a CRM gadget

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the catalog">
    On a dashboard or on your home page, click **Add gadget**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a CRM gadget">
    Choose **CRM** in the list of apps and click the gadget you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust it">
    Open the gadget settings to change the filter, the columns or the grouping. A gadget can also take a saved filter as its source.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The gadgets

| Gadget | Shows | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **Pipeline** | Open opportunities by stage, with the sum of the amounts per currency. | Read right on opportunities |
| **Won and lost** | Opportunities closed this month, split into won and lost, with the sum per currency. | Read right on opportunities |
| **Due opportunities** | Open opportunities that close by the end of this month, the earliest first. | Read right on opportunities |
| **My CRM tasks** | Your open CRM tasks, by due date. | Read right on tasks |
| **New companies** | Companies created this month against the month before. | Read right on companies |

Sums are never added across currencies: each currency has its own column.

## What a gadget shows you

A gadget reads the CRM with your own rights. It counts and adds up only the records your CRM roles allow you to see, and it does not offer a gadget at all when your roles may not read the type. If your roles hide the amount field, a gadget that adds up amounts cannot show its sum.

The month in **Won and lost** and **Due opportunities** follows the time zone you set in your profile.
