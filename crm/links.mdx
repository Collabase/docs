description: "Attach a project, a page or a registry object to a company, person or opportunity, and turn an opportunity into a work item."

---

The CRM stands on its own. When you can also use Projects, Pages or the registry, a **Linked items** section appears at the bottom of the **Overview** tab of a record. Without these apps the section is not there, and the CRM works as before.

## Attach an item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the section">
    Open a company, person or opportunity and find **Linked items**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the kind">
    Click **Link a project**, **Link a page** or **Link a registry object**. Each button appears only for the apps you can use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the item">
    Search by name and click the item.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You need the right to change the record. The search offers only what you may see in the other app, and the link follows the rules of that app: someone who may not open a project sees **Not accessible** instead of its name.

When the project, work item, page or registry object is deleted, the link disappears with it. A target that exists but that you may not open stays as **Not accessible**.

To remove a link, click the cross next to it. Removing a link does not change the project, page or registry object.

## Turn an opportunity into a work item

On an opportunity, click **Create work item**, then pick the project. The work item is named after the opportunity, and its description links back to the opportunity. The link stays on the opportunity under **Linked items**.

The CRM creates no project. You need the right to add work items in the project you pick, and a project you cannot see is not offered.

On the work item, the **CRM** panel lists the records it is linked to. It shows only records you may see in the CRM.
