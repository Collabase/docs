When you open the CRM, it takes you to the first entry of the sidebar that your CRM roles let you see, usually **Companies**. The start page appears only when your roles let you see no list at all, for example while an administrator has not yet given your role any rights.

## What the start page shows

| Part | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Your CRM is ready** | A note that companies, people and deals appear as soon as they are created. |
| **Meetings to assign** | Meetings of yours that the CRM could not place on a record. The section is there only when you have such meetings and the Meetings app is available to you. |

Under **Meetings to assign**, click **Confirm** to attach a meeting to a record or **Create company** for a guest from an unknown company domain. See [Meetings from the CRM](/crm/meetings#suggestions) for what each choice does.

## If you see only the start page

You hold the app but your roles show no object type. Ask a CRM admin to add you to a role, see [CRM roles and settings](/crm/roles). A person without any CRM role sees nothing of the CRM, and its address answers "not found".
