A saved view stores how a list looks: the filter, the sort order and the columns. Each list of the CRM has its own views, shown as tabs above the table. **All** is the list without a view.

## Open a view

Click a tab. The list takes the filter, the sort order and the columns of the view. You can narrow the view further with the filter bar. The tab stays selected, and the buttons **Save as view** and **Update view** appear because the list now differs from the saved view.

## Save a view

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up the list">
    Filter, sort and choose the columns as you want the view to look.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    Click **Save as view**, enter a name and click **Save**. The button appears only when the list differs from the open view, or from the standard list if no view is open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide who sees it">
    A new view is private. Switch on **Visible to everyone who uses the CRM** to share it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change, share or delete a view

Only the person who created a view can change or delete it. Everyone else can open a shared view but not edit it.

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| **Update** | Open the view, change the list, then click **Update view**. |
| **Rename or share** | Open the view and click **Edit view**. |
| **Delete** | Open the view and click **Delete view**. The view disappears for everyone it is shared with. The records stay as they are. |

## What a shared view shows

A shared view shows each person only the records and fields their own CRM role allows. A field your role hides is not offered in the filter, and a view that filters or groups by such a field is not saved.
