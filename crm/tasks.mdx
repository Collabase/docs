A CRM task works like a task in a project: it has a title, a description, a status, a due date and assignees. It does not belong to a project or a space, so the CRM needs neither.

## Create a task

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the tab">
    Open a company, a person or a deal and choose the **Tasks** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the task">
    Choose **New task**, enter a title and fill in what you know.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Choose **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **New task** button appears only when your CRM role allows you to create tasks. A task sits on the record you started from.

## Change a task

- **Status and due date** change right in the list. Choose the status pill or the date.
- **Everything else** opens in a dialog when you choose the title: title, description, assignees and the delete action.
- Someone may change a task when they created it, are assigned to it, or hold a CRM role that allows changing tasks and can see the task.
- If a colleague changed the task while you were editing it, Collabase tells you and keeps their version. Open the task again to see it.

A due date in the past is allowed. The task is marked **Overdue** until it is done.

## My tasks

**My tasks** in the CRM sidebar lists the open tasks assigned to you, soonest due first. It shows CRM tasks only.

## Who sees a task

- The person who created a task and the people assigned to it always see it.
- Everyone else sees it only when their roles allow them to read every record the task is on.
- A record you may not read appears as **Not accessible**, without its name.
- When a record is deleted, the task stays with its creator and assignees. When every record of a task is in the trash, only the creator and assignees still see it. Restoring a record brings the task back for everyone.

## Notices

| When | Who | How |
|---|---|---|
| Someone assigns you a task | The new assignee, not the person who made the change | In the inbox |
| A task is due today | Each assignee, at 7:00 in their own time zone | In the inbox, and by email when you switched that channel on |

If several of your tasks are due on the same day, you get one notice. Notices only reach people who may open the record. Change the channels under **Profile → Notifications**, category **CRM**.

<Note>
The due notice needs the background worker. Without it, Collabase reports the CRM task notices as degraded in the health check.
</Note>
