A list collects companies or people for one purpose, for example a Christmas gift action. Every entry can carry values in columns you define, such as **Gift: yes/no**. Lists are in the CRM sidebar under **Lists**.

## Create a list

Click **New list** on the **Lists** page. Enter a name, choose whether the list holds companies or people, and choose how it is filled.

| Filled | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Hand-picked** | You add records yourself. Entries stay until you remove them. |
| **By filter** | The list holds every record that matches a query, for example `owner = currentUser()`. Records enter and leave as they start or stop matching. |

You need the right to create lists. A new list is private until you share it.

## Add records

Open a hand-picked list and click **Add records**. Search by title, switch on **Mine** to narrow to the records you own, tick the ones you want and click **Add**. Adding a record twice leaves one entry. A record you may not read is not offered.

## Add columns and set values

Click **Columns** and add a column with a name and a type: text, number, date, choice or yes/no. A choice column takes its options one per line. Rename a column by clicking its name. Deleting a column removes its values from every entry, so the CRM asks first.

Set a value directly in the table: type into the cell, pick a choice or tick the box. A date is a day without a time. A value the column does not accept is refused and the cell shows its old value again. In a list filled by filter, the first value you set creates the entry for that record.

## Share a list

Click **Share** and pick a person. Switch on **May edit** to let them add records and set values. Everyone sees only the records their own CRM role allows, also in your list. Remove a person with the cross next to their name.

## Groups in the sidebar

Click **New group** under the lists in the sidebar and name it. Drag a list onto a group to move it, or use the menu next to the list and choose **Move to group**. Drag it onto the area below the groups, or choose **Top level**, to take it out again. Click a group name to collapse it. Deleting a group moves its lists to the top level.

Only the owner of a list can move, rename, share or delete it.
