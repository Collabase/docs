The CRM opens on its first list. **Companies**, **People** and **Opportunities** each have a list in the sidebar. Admins also find the model, the roles, the settings and the import there. The sidebar shows only the lists your CRM roles allow you to read. A list you may not read is missing from the sidebar, and its address answers "not found".

## Work with a list

Each list shows the first 50 rows at once. Click **Load more** under the table to add the next 50.

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| **Filter** | Use the filter bar above the table. Pick a field, such as **Owner**, and the values to keep. The text field searches names, domains, email addresses and phone numbers. The filter is part of the address, so a link shows the same result. |
| **Sort** | Click a column header. A second click reverses the order. |
| **Choose columns** | Click **Columns**, then add, remove or reorder columns. You can also drag a header to move its column. Your choice is saved for your account and follows you to every device. |

The columns you can add are limited to the fields of that list. A field your roles hide is not offered, and it does not appear in the filter bar either.

### Standard columns

| List | Columns |
|---|---|
| **Companies** | Name, Domains, Owner |
| **People** | Name, Email addresses |

## Create a company or a person

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new item">
    Click **New** above the list. The button appears only if your CRM roles allow you to create items of this type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the boxes">
    For a company, enter the company name and, if you know it, the domain. For a person, enter a first name, a last name or both, and optionally an email address. All other fields are filled in later on the item itself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Click **Create**. The new item appears in the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A domain or an email address belongs to one item only. If another company already holds the domain, or another person the email address, the CRM refuses the new item and tells you so.
