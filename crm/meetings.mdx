Meetings and the CRM work together when both are available to you. A meeting with a customer shows on the timeline of the company and the person, you can create a meeting from a record, and the company page shows a briefing before the next meeting.

Without the Meetings app none of this appears, and the CRM works as before. See [Meetings](/meetings/overview) for planning, polls and meeting notes.

## How a meeting finds its record {#matching}

When a meeting ends or changes its guests, the CRM matches the guests with its records. Only guests without a Collabase account count. Each guest is decided on its own:

| The guest | What happens |
|---|---|
| Has an email address that exactly one record holds | The meeting is attached to that record, confirmed |
| Has no such address, but the domain belongs to exactly one company | The meeting is attached to that company, confirmed |
| Fits several records | The meeting waits as a suggestion for each of them |
| Fits no record, and the domain is a company domain | The meeting waits as a suggestion to create the company |
| Uses a private mail provider such as Gmail and no record holds the address | Nothing. A private provider is matched by the exact address only |

A meeting with guests from several companies appears on each of them. An attached meeting shows as an entry on the [timeline](/crm/timeline) of the record. A meeting appears only for people who may open the meeting and read the record.

## Assign a meeting the CRM could not place {#suggestions}

Where a guest fits several records or none, the organizer of the meeting gets a notice and the meeting waits.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the record">
    Open the company or person on the **Overview** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the section">
    The section **Meetings to assign** lists each suggested meeting with its date and the guest address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide">
    Click **Confirm** to attach the meeting to this record, or **Not this one** to remove the suggestion.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you organize the meeting, the CRM home page also lists your waiting suggestions. For a guest from an unknown company domain, **Create company** creates the company with that domain and attaches the meeting at once.

**Confirm** makes the link manual. Later changes of the guests never remove or replace a manual link. The section does not appear when no meeting waits. Each meeting entry on the timeline has **Create task** for a follow-up on the record.

## Create a meeting from a company or a person {#create-meeting}

You need the right to read the record and the right to create meetings. Without the right to read the record, the address answers "not found".

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the record">
    Open a company or a person. **Create meeting** is in the head of the page, next to the owner. It is missing where you may not create meetings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the meeting">
    Enter a title, the date, the time and the length in minutes. Date and time count in your own time zone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the guests">
    Switch **Invite guests** on and tick the guests. For a person these are their own email addresses, for a company the addresses of the people who work there. Only addresses you may see on the record are offered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Click **Create**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| **Invite guests** is | Result |
|---|---|
| Off | The meeting stays a draft with you as the only participant. Nobody receives an invitation. |
| On | The meeting is planned and the invitations go out. |

The meeting is attached to the record at once and counts as confirmed. For a person it is also attached to the main company, for a company to the people you invited. It shows on the timeline of each.

If the meeting was created but could not be attached, a message says so. Confirm the link in the meeting afterwards. Trying again never creates a second meeting.

## The briefing before a meeting {#briefing}

On the **Overview** tab of a company, the section **Briefing for upcoming meetings** shows the next meetings that hang on the company, up to five.

| Who looks | What the section shows |
|---|---|
| The organizer, when a written briefing exists | The written overview, written about an hour before the start, and below it the list of sources |
| Everyone else | The list of sources only |
| The organizer, when no written briefing exists (the AI is off, or the meeting is less than an hour away) | The list of sources only |

The list of sources is what the CRM knows about the records of the meeting: the company or person with domain and owner, open tasks, open opportunities and the titles of notes that everyone may read. It holds only what you may read yourself.

It never contains mail, amounts, private or shared notes, or the text of a note. If the AI may not read the content of a type, its records appear without names. The written briefing uses the organizer's rights and the organizer's AI budget. See [AI and privacy](/meetings/ai-and-privacy#ai-briefing-before-a-meeting) for when it is written and how to switch it off.

## Troubleshooting {#troubleshooting}

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **This record holds no email address to invite.** | Add an email address to the person, or to a person of the company, and open the dialog again. |
| **The guests could not be loaded.** | Close the dialog and open it again. If it persists, reload the page. |
| **The meeting was created, but could not be attached to the record. Confirm the link in the meeting.** | Open the meeting and confirm the record there. Do not create the meeting again. |
| **{count} address(es) were not invited.** | The address was not valid, was listed twice, was already on the meeting or went over the limit of guests. Check the guests in the meeting. |
| **The meeting could not be created.** | Check title, date and time, then try again. |
| A meeting you expect is missing on the record | You may not open the meeting or may not read the record, or the guest address matches no record. Look under **Meetings to assign**. |
