A note is a piece of text on one or more records. Open the **Notes** tab on a record, click **New note**, give it a title and write the text. The text uses the same editor as pages, so you can format it, add links and mention colleagues.

The tab is shown to everyone whose CRM roles let them read notes. The button **New note** appears only if your roles also let you write them.

## Who can read a note {#visibility}

Every note has one of three levels. Choose it under the text, and change it later at any time.

| Level | Who reads the note |
|---|---|
| **Everyone who can read the records** | Anyone who may read every record the note is on. This is the default. |
| **Only people I choose** | The people and groups you add, and you. |
| **Only me** | You alone. Nobody else sees it, administrators and system administrators included. There is no emergency access. |

A note on several records reaches a reader only if they may read **all** of them. If you may read the company but not the opportunity the note is also on, you do not see it, and nothing tells you that it exists.

A private note never shows up for others in lists, search, the timeline, exports or the AI assistant. The number of notes you see is the number you may read, so the count says nothing about notes of others.

## Mentions {#mentions}

Type **@** and a name to mention a colleague. They get a notification in the **Mentions** category, but only if they may read the note. The notification shows the title of the note and never its text. Someone you already mentioned is not notified again when you edit the note.

In a private note a mention is kept but tells nobody. The editor says so under the text.

## Change and delete {#edit}

Only the author changes a note, and only with the right to write notes. Click the pencil on the note, change it and click **Save**. Nothing else is needed: there is no dialog.

If a colleague saved the note after you opened it, your save is refused. Your text stays in the editor, the latest version is loaded, and a note asks you to check it and save again.

The author deletes their own note. Anyone else can delete a note that is not private if their roles let them delete notes. Deleting asks for one confirmation.

## What is logged {#audit}

Changing the level of a note, and adding or removing the people it is shared with, is recorded in the audit log. The entry holds the new level and the number of people, not the title, the text or their names. Changing the text is not logged.
