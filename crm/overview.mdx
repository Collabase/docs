<Note>
The CRM is an experimental app. It is off on a standard installation until an administrator switches it on or a person opts in under **Profile → User settings → Collabase Lab**.
</Note>

The CRM is its own app next to Projects and Service Management. It holds companies, people and deals, each with notes, tasks and a timeline. It runs without Projects, Pages or Spaces.

## Who can use it {#who}

An administrator gives the app to groups in **Administration → Groups → App access**, like any other app:

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| User | Open the app and work with the records the person's CRM roles allow |
| Admin | Also manage the CRM model, roles and settings |

The app appears in the app drawer only for people who hold it. A person without the app does not see it, and its address answers "not found". An installation without the CRM turned on answers the same way to everyone.

## Search

The global search and the search in the header find companies, people, deals, notes and tasks. They appear as the kinds **CRM records**, **CRM notes** and **CRM tasks**, and each result opens the record on the tab where the note or task lives. A note is found by its title, a task by its title too.

Search shows only what the CRM shows you. A type your roles may not read gives no result and no count, and a field your roles hide cannot be searched. A private note is found by its author alone, a shared note by the people it is shared with, and every other note by the people who may read all of its records. A task is found by its assignees and its creator, and by everyone who may read all of its records. Administrators have no extra view.

## Notifications

The CRM has its own notification category, **CRM**. By default CRM notifications arrive in the inbox only, not by email. Each person changes this under **Profile → Notifications**.
