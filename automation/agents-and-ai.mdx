AI in Automation is optional. Without an AI model on your instance or in the workspace, flows that use AI cannot be published, and everything else keeps working.

## What you can do

| You want to | Use |
|---|---|
| Summarise, classify or draft text in a flow | The **Ask the model** step. It sends one question and returns the answer as text. |
| Let a model work out the steps to reach a goal | The [Agent step](/automation/agent-step), with [tools](/automation/agent-tools). |
| Choose which model is used and set a budget | The [AI provider](/automation/ai-provider) of the workspace. |
| Describe a step in a sentence, or ask what went wrong in a run | [Build with AI](/automation/build-with-ai). |
| Use your flows from Claude, Cursor or your own agent | The [MCP server](/automation/mcp-server) of the workspace. |
| Let people talk to a flow in a browser | The [chat page](/automation/chat-page). |

## Safe by design

- An agent can only do what its tools allow, and a tool can ask a person first.
- Text from outside, such as the data a flow was started with or what a search returns, is handed to the model as data and not as instructions.
- Calls to the model go out from Collabase. A plug of the sandbox never sees the key of your provider.
- The assistants never publish a flow.
