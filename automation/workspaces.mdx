An Automation workspace holds the flows of one team. It has its own members, connections, runs, audit trail, analytics, AI provider and Git connection. A service account named after the Space carries out what its flows do, so every change a flow makes in Collabase is recorded as made by that account.

## Roles

Each member or group has one role in a Space. A person gets the highest role of their own and of their groups. System administrators are administrators of every Space.

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Viewer** | Read flows, runs and the version history, and export a flow. |
| **Editor** | Build, test and publish flows, switch them on and off, start runs, retry runs and manage connections. |
| **Admin** | Everything an editor can, plus members, settings, the audit trail, analytics, the AI provider, Git sync and deleting the Space. |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the workspace">
    In **Studio**, select **New Space** and give it a name and an emoji.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add members">
    Open the **Members** tab, add people or groups and choose a role for each.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sensitive Spaces

Switch on **Sensitive** under **Settings** when a flow in the Space can do harm if it is wrong. Publishing a flow or switching it on then needs a second person.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for approval">
    The editor selects **Ask for approval** instead of **Publish**. The other admins of the Space get a notification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide">
    An admin opens the request and approves or rejects it. The decision must come from an admin who did not ask and did not make the last change to the draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Result">
    An approval publishes the flow or switches it on. A rejection changes nothing. The editor is told either way.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If anyone changes the draft after the request, the request is withdrawn, so nobody approves something they did not see. The editor can also withdraw a request.

## Run retention

Finished runs and their logs are kept for 1 to 365 days, 30 by default. Set it under **Settings**. Runs that are still going are never removed.

## Audit trail

Admins see the **Audit** tab: who created, changed, published or deleted what in the Space, and who decided an approval, changed a connection or connected Git. An entry never contains a secret or the content of a flow.

## Analytics

Admins see the **Analytics** tab, and system administrators see all Spaces under **Administration, Studio analytics**. The page shows active flows, successful and failed runs per day, average duration, the most used plugs, who builds, agent steps and AI tokens. The figures are up to an hour old. The same figures are available as the **Studio analytics** dashboard gadget.

## AI provider

By default flows use the AI model of your instance. Admins can set a provider for the Space instead under **Settings**, with an optional monthly token budget. See [AI and agents](/automation/agents-and-ai).
