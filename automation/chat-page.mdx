A flow that starts from a chat message can be offered on a page of its own. Every message starts a run, and the flow's reply appears in the same conversation.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start from a chat message">
    Choose **Chat message received** as the trigger. The trigger data holds the visitor's `message` and a `sessionId`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reply">
    Add **Reply in the chat** and write the reply. You can use the output of earlier steps, for example the answer of an AI step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and switch on">
    Only a published flow that is switched on answers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make the link">
    Select **Chat link**, then **Make link**, and share the address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- A visitor's conversation belongs to their browser tab. A reply that takes longer than 30 seconds shows a notice and appears in the conversation when it is ready.
- One address can send 100 messages a minute.
- Messages and replies are kept with the run and removed with it.
- **Make a new link** replaces the link at once. The old address shows a not found page.
- Only text is supported.
