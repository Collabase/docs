**Home** is the first page after you sign in. It answers two questions: what is waiting for you, and what changed in your Spaces. The layout is the same for everyone and cannot be configured.

## What Home shows {#sections}

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Greeting | Your number of active work items, with tiles for **Work Items** (open and assigned to you), **Overdue**, **Due This Week** (the next seven days) and **Projects** (projects you are a member of) |
| Quick create | **New Page**, **Templates**, and tiles to open your Spaces |
| **Active Spaces** | The Spaces you work in |
| **My Recent Work** | The last five pages you opened |
| **Your Favorites** | The pages you starred. Hidden while you have none |
| **Team Activity** | The pages in your Spaces most recently updated, written by someone else. Hidden when there are none |
| **My Tasks** | Your open work items in all projects, the earliest due date first |

<Tip>
  **My Tasks** is the fastest way to see your work across every project. Click a row to open the work item.
</Tip>

## Mark a page as a favorite {#favorites}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    Open any page in Collabase Docs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Star it">
    Click the star in the page header (**Add to favorites**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find it on Home">
    The page appears under **Your Favorites** on Home, the most recently starred first. Click the star again (**Remove from favorites**) to remove it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only pages can be starred. To keep a set of work items at hand, save a search as a [filter](/concepts/filters#save).
