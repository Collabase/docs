Space settings control how one Space is set up and who can work in it. Open the Space and go to its settings. The settings open only for people with the right to manage the Space's settings, which the **Space Admin** role holds; everyone else gets a "not found" page. See [Permissions](/concepts/permissions#space-roles).

The navigation groups the settings into three areas:

| Group | Entries |
|---|---|
| **Space** | **General**, **Translation**, **Features**, and **Integrations** when the GitHub integration is switched on |
| **Access** | **Members**, **Roles & permissions**, **Visibility**, **Zones** |
| **Content** | **Content map**, **Custom Fields**, **Archived pages** |

## General {#general}

**General** holds the name, icon and owner of the Space.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Space Name** | The name shown in the space switcher and the header. Click the icon next to it to pick another icon. |
| **Space owner** | The person who has full administrative access to the Space |

Click **Save Changes**. Renaming the Space does not change its address, so existing links keep working, see [Spaces](/concepts/spaces).

The **Danger Zone** at the bottom of **General** deletes the Space, see [Delete a Space](#delete).

## Translation {#translation}

**Translation** sets the **Default language (source)** of the Space and the **Allowed target languages** its pages may be translated into. See [Choose the languages of a Space](/docs/translation#space-languages).

## Features {#features}

The **Features** tab, under **Space** in the settings navigation, turns apps on or off for this Space. It shapes the Space sidebar for all members.

| Switch | What it provides |
|

## Members {#members}

**Members** lists the people with direct access to the Space and the groups that reach it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a member">
    Click **Add Member**. The dialog **Add space member** opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the person and the role">
    Under **User**, search for a person who already has a Collabase account. Under **Role**, pick the space role, then click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change a member's role, pick another role in the row. To remove a member, click the trash icon in the row and confirm with **Remove member**. The person loses access to the Space at once and can be added again later. The owner's row cannot be changed here; change the owner under [General](#general).

The section **Inherited group access** shows the groups that have access to this Space, with their role and number of members. It is read-only here. An administrator grants a group access in **Admin → Groups**, on the group's **Space Access** tab, see [Users & Groups](/admin/users-groups).

## Roles & permissions {#roles-and-permissions}

**Roles & permissions** lists the space roles with the number of members in each, and the **Permission scheme** that decides what each role may do.

Under **Active scheme**, pick a scheme and click **Save**. **No scheme (default model)** applies the built-in role model. Click **View and edit scheme permissions** to open the scheme itself. Schemes are created and changed under **Admin → Permission Schemes**, see [Permissions](/concepts/permissions#permission-schemes).

## Visibility {#visibility}

**Visibility** decides who can read the Space without being a member.

| Switch | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Make space public** | Every signed-in user can read pages in the Space without being a member. A public Space can show its blog posts as news in the Intranet; **Open intranet settings** leads there. |
| **Allow viewer suggestions** | People without edit access can propose changes that are then reviewed, see [Suggestions](/docs/suggestions) |

## Zones {#zones}

**Zones** lists the **Restricted zones** of the Space: folders and pages with their own access list. From the list you can make a zone public again. See [Find all restricted content in a Space](/docs/page-restrictions#zones).

## Content map {#content-map}

**Content map** shows the page structure of the Space and moves pages to another place or another Space. See [Restructure a Space from the content map](/docs/organize-pages#content-map).

## Custom Fields {#custom-fields}

**Custom Fields** turns field collections on or off for the pages of this Space. The collections themselves are created by an administrator, see [Custom fields](/admin/custom-fields#create-collection).

### Default values {#default-values}

Below the collections, **Default values** sets what a new work item in this Space starts with: priority, work item type, assignee, labels and the custom fields that apply to the work item type. Save the section with **Save**; **Discard changes** returns to the saved values.

A new work item takes its values in this order:

- First, what you set yourself while creating it. A field you clear in the create form stays empty.
- Then the default value of the Space.
- Then the default value of the custom field itself, set by an administrator.
- Last, the standard value (priority Medium).

The defaults apply wherever work items are created: the create form, quick add, subtasks, the API, mail, meetings, automations, the service portal and plugins. They do not apply when you copy or restore a work item, or when you change the type of an existing one.

A default that no longer fits is skipped and the work item is still created: an assignee who cannot reach the project, a deleted label or a removed option. Dates need a fixed day such as `2026-12-31`; a relative value such as `+7d` is not accepted. A service portal request goes to the default assignee when the Space has one, and to the person who filed it otherwise.

Only people who can manage the custom fields of the Space may change the defaults; everyone else sees the saved values. Every change is recorded in the audit log.

## Archived pages {#archived-pages}

**Archived pages** lists the pages archived in this Space. Restore a page, or delete it for good. See [Restore an archived page](/docs/organize-pages#archived-pages).

## Integrations {#integrations}

**Integrations** appears when the GitHub integration is switched on. It connects the Space to GitHub, see [Connect a Space](/projects/github#connect-a-space).

## AI settings {#ai}

Collabase AI is configured for the whole instance, not per Space. See [AI configuration](/admin/ai-configuration).

## Delete a Space {#delete}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the danger zone">
    Open **General** and scroll to **Danger Zone**. Under **Delete this space**, click **Delete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with the name">
    Type the space name exactly and click **Delete space**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Deleting needs the right to archive the Space, which the **Space Admin** role holds.

<Warning>
  The dialog says the Space and all its projects, work items, pages and files are deleted permanently. In fact the Space moves to the trash of the instance first. It stays there until an administrator restores it or deletes it for good; there is no expiry date. Ask your administrator at once if you deleted a Space by mistake. See [Trash](/admin/backup#trash) and [Data retention](/admin/data-retention).
</Warning>
