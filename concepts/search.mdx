Collabase has two ways to search. The header search answers "where is that page or work item?" in a few keystrokes. The **Search** page lists every match with sorting and paging, and it is where you build [filters with the query language](/concepts/filters). Both only show content you have access to.

## Header search {#header-search}

Click **Search...** in the header, or press `Cmd + K` (Mac) or `Ctrl + K` (Windows and Linux). Results appear as you type, grouped by kind:

| Group | Contains |
|---|---|
| **Spaces** | Spaces by name or mission |
| **Projects** | Projects, work items and Registry objects |
| Pages | Pages of Collabase Docs |

Use the arrow keys to move through results and `Enter` to open one. To see every match, click **View all results** at the bottom of the panel.

### Narrow the header search

Click **Filter** to limit results to an **Author**, a **Label** or a **Space**. **Reset** removes the filter.

### Before you type

With an empty search field, the panel shows **Recently viewed**: the last six items you opened from this search panel, stored in your browser. **Quick actions** links to the full search and to **Settings**.

## The Search page {#search-page}

The Search page searches across spaces, pages, projects, work items, Registry objects and the other content types listed under **Type**. Enter at least two characters.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Search page">
    In the header search, click **View all results**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your terms">
    Type in **Search across all entities…** and click **Search**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow by type">
    Under **Filters**, pick a **Type** to search only one kind of content, for example projects, test runs or automation rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sort the results">
    Choose **Relevance**, **Recently updated**, **Oldest update**, **Newest first** or **Oldest first**. Results come 20 per page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If nothing matches, the page shows **No results for "…"**. Try other words or a different type.

### How words are matched {#matching}

| Content | Matching |
|---|---|
| Pages | Every word must appear. Each word also matches longer words that start with it, so "onboard" finds "onboarding" |
| Spaces, projects, work items | The text appears anywhere in the name or title |

For precise conditions, such as "assigned to me and overdue", switch the filter to **Advanced** and write a query. See [Filters and query language](/concepts/filters).

## Search and permissions

Results only include content in Spaces and projects you can open. If you expect a result and do not see it, it may be in a Space you are not a member of. Ask a Space admin for access.

## Search with CollabaseAI {#semantic-search}

Questions in plain language, such as "What did we decide about API versioning?", go to [CollabaseAI](/concepts/collabase-ai). When an administrator enables <Tooltip tip="Search by meaning instead of exact words. CollabaseAI uses it to find the passages that answer a question." cta="Glossary" href="/glossary#semantic-search">semantic search</Tooltip> in the [AI settings](/admin/ai-configuration), CollabaseAI finds the relevant pages, work item descriptions and comments by meaning and answers with links to them. The header search and the Search page match words, not meaning.
