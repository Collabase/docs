Collabase tells you about the things that need you: a mention, a task, a page someone shared with you. Notifications collect in the notification center at the top of the screen, and some also arrive by email.

## The notification center {#notification-center}

Click the bell (**Notifications**) at the top right of the screen. A number on the bell shows how many notifications you have not read.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **All** / **Unread** | Shows every notification, or only the unread ones |
| **Mark all as read** | Marks every notification as read |
| **Mark as read** | Marks one notification as read without opening it |
| Click a notification | Opens the page, work item or Space it is about and marks it as read |

The center shows your newest 50 notifications and checks for new ones every minute. An empty list shows **You're all caught up.**, or **No unread notifications** when the **Unread** filter is on.

## What notifies you {#triggers}

| Event | Notification | Also by email |
|---|---|---|
| Someone mentions you in a [comment on a page](/docs/comments#mentions) | "mentioned you in a discussion" | Yes |
| Someone starts a [discussion](/docs/comments#inline-comments) on a page you wrote and does not mention you in it | "commented on" the page | No |
| Someone replies in a [discussion](/docs/comments#discussions) you took part in, and does not mention you in the reply | "replied in a discussion" on the page | No |
| Someone assigns you a to-do in a page by mentioning you in it, see [Assign a to-do](/docs/comments#to-dos) | **Task Assigned** | No |
| Someone adds you to the access list of a [private folder](/docs/page-restrictions#make-private) | **Page shared with you** | No |
| Someone suggests a change to a page you wrote, see [Suggestions](/docs/suggestions) | A new suggestion | No |
| Someone adds you to a Space | **Added to Space** | No |
| Someone [shares a dashboard](/concepts/share-dashboards) with you or a group you are in | **Dashboard shared with you** | No |
| Someone mentions you in a [comment on a dashboard gadget](/concepts/dashboards#comments) | "mentioned you in a comment on a gadget" | Yes |
| You are invited to a meeting, the meeting changes or it is cancelled | An entry for each, with the calendar email as before | The calendar email, not a second one |
| Slack or Teams stops working | **The Slack connection stopped working** (administrators), **Check your Slack connection** (the person) | Administrators |
| A test run is assigned to you | **Test run assigned** | Yes |
| A test run you are assigned to is complete and cases failed | **Test run finished with failures** | Yes |
| Someone adds you to an Automation workspace | **You were added to the Automation workspace** | No |
| The last admin of an Automation workspace leaves the instance | **Automation workspace without an admin** (system administrators) | No |
| Someone shares a roadmap with you or a group you are in | **Roadmap shared with you** | No |
| Someone makes you the owner of an initiative | **You own the initiative** | Yes |
| The planned end of an initiative you own has passed | **The initiative is overdue** | Yes |
| A project or portfolio linked to your initiative falls outside its period, is closed or shows a red light | **The plan of the initiative has a conflict** | Yes |
| Status reports of your projects are due | **Status reports due**, one notice with the count and up to five project names | Yes |
| Portfolio reports you own are due | **Portfolio reports due**, one notice with the count and up to five portfolio names | Yes |
| A portfolio report is submitted and you can review it | **Portfolio report awaits review** | Yes |
| Your portfolio report is approved or returned | **Portfolio report approved** or **returned** | Yes |
| A plan export you requested is ready | **Your plan export is ready**, with the download link | No |
| A customer replies to a ticket of a [service project](/service-management/replies) assigned to you, or to an unassigned ticket of a queue you watch | **The customer replied** | Yes |
| A new ticket lands in a [queue](/service-management/queues) you watch | **New ticket in a queue**, with **Assign to me** in the inbox | No |
| As a customer: you submit a request in the [portal](/service-management/portal) | **We received your request**, with its key (email only) | Yes |
| As a customer: a colleague or the service team shares a request with you by name (not when it is shared with your whole organization) | **A request was shared with you**, with its key (email only) | Yes |
| As a customer: the team gives a public reply to your request, or resolves or closes it. Internal notes never reach you | **New reply to your request**, **Your request is resolved** (email only) | Yes |
| The SLA clock of a ticket in a [service project](/service-management/sla) is about to run out. It reaches the assignee, or the people who watch a queue of the project while nobody is assigned | **SLA for the ticket is about to run out** | Yes |
| The SLA clock of a ticket runs out. The people who manage the project are told as well | **SLA for the ticket breached** | Yes |
| A ticket waits for your [approval](/service-management/approvals) as an internal approver | **Approval needed**, with **Approve** and **Reject** in the inbox | Yes |
| The approval of a ticket assigned to you is decided | **The ticket was approved** or **rejected** | No |
| As a customer admin of an organization: a colleague's request waits for your approval | **Approval needed**, decided in the [portal](/service-management/portal) | Yes |
| As a customer: the approval of your request is decided | **Your request was approved** or **rejected** | Yes |
| Events in projects, such as a work item assigned to you or a comment on it | See [Tasks](/projects/tasks) | Depends on the event |

Mentions in the running text of a page do not notify anyone. Use a comment or a to-do item when the person should react.

A notification about a comment opens the page at the discussion. You are never notified about your own comments, about a discussion that is resolved or reopened, or about a page in the trash. The notification center groups notifications about the same page.

## Emails about your pages {#page-emails}

When someone else changes the content of a page you created, you receive an email with a link to the page. The email is sent in your preferred language.

Your administrator can switch this email off for the whole instance in the mail settings of the administration area. There is no personal setting for it in this version.

## Watching pages {#watching}

Collabase has no watch or follow function for single pages or Spaces in this version. You hear about a page when you created it, when you are mentioned in a comment or a to-do, when it is shared with you, or when someone suggests a change to it.

To keep important pages at hand, mark them as favorites, see [Pages](/docs/pages#favorites).
