A saved filter is yours until you share it. Sharing gives others the **query**, not your results: everyone who opens the filter sees the work items, pages and other objects **they** may see.

## Share a filter {#share}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the filter">
    Open the filter from **My Filters** and click **Share**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who">
    Pick **Person**, **Group**, **Space** or **Project**, click **Add** and search by name. **Everyone signed in** adds all people with an account who are not guests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what they may do">
    Each entry is **Can use** or **Can edit**. Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Access | What the person can do |
|---|---|
| **Can use** | Run the filter, star it for themselves, save a copy under their own name |
| **Can edit** | Everything above, and change the query, the columns and the sort order of the shared filter |

Only the owner shares a filter, hands it over or deletes it. Someone with **Can edit** cannot do these.

If a person has several entries, for example directly and through a group, the higher access counts.

## Who can be chosen {#targets}

- **Space and project:** you can choose only the ones you see yourself. A Space or project shares the filter with everyone who may see it, including people who join later.
- **Person and group:** any active person or group.
- **Guests:** a guest can be named as a person and then opens the filter. A guest is not reached through a group, a Space or **Everyone signed in**, and cannot share a filter.

When you share with a person or a group, the people it reaches get a notification. Sharing with a Space, a project or everyone sends none, so a large audience is not flooded. Raising **Can use** to **Can edit** sends none either.

Removing an entry takes effect with the next request: the person no longer finds the filter.

## Hand a filter over {#hand-over}

The owner can hand a filter to a person who can already open it, for example before leaving a team. After the handover the previous owner keeps only the access the filter gives them through its entries.

## When the owner is removed {#orphaned}

When a person is deleted from Collabase, their **private** filters are deleted with them. Filters they **shared** stay, with **(deleted user)** as owner, and everyone they were shared with keeps using them. People with **Can edit** can still change the query, but nobody can share it further.

Administrators open **Administration → Infrastructure → Search filters**. The list shows every filter with its owner and how it is shared, and **Only filters without an owner** narrows it to filters that need a new owner. An administrator hands a shared filter to a person who can open it, or deletes it. The query of a private filter stays hidden from administrators.

Administrators with an account receive a notification each time a removed person leaves shared filters behind.
