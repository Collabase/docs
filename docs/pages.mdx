A page is the unit of Collabase Docs: a document in a <Tooltip tip="The top-level container in Collabase, with its own members, apps and content." cta="Glossary" href="/glossary#space">Space</Tooltip> that you write in a rich-text editor, share with the members of that Space and find again later. This page covers what you can create, how editing and saving work, and the information every page carries.

## What you can create {#page-types}

The **Create** button at the top of the screen offers three kinds of content for Collabase Docs. All three sit in the same page tree.

| Item | What it is | Opens after creation |
|---|---|---|
| **Page** | A rich-text document with headings, lists, tables, macros and comments | In edit mode |
| **Whiteboard** | A drawing board for sketches, diagrams and wireframes | In edit mode |
| **Folder** | A container that groups pages. A folder can carry its own access list, see [Restrict access to pages](/docs/page-restrictions) | Stays in the tree |

The same menu also offers **Create Work Item** when the Space has Projects switched on. The Docs entries appear only when **Documents** is switched on for the Space, see [Spaces](/concepts/spaces#turn-modules-on-or-off).

## Create a page {#create-a-page}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Space">
    Pick the Space in the space switcher at the top of the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the page">
    Click **Create**, then **Page**. The page is created at the top level of the Space and opens in edit mode.

    You can also click **Docs** in the left rail and then the **Create Page** button at the top of the page tree. To create a page inside a folder, open the folder and click **Create Page** there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it a title">
    Click the title in the bar at the top of the page and type. A page without a title shows as **Untitled** in the tree.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write">
    Type `/` to insert a block or macro. See [Editor elements](/docs/editor-elements) for everything you can insert.
  </Step>
</Steps>

New pages land at the top level. To place a page under another page, drag it in the tree, see [Organize pages](/docs/organize-pages#reorder-and-nest).

To start from a template instead of a blank page, see [Templates](/docs/templates).

## Edit and save {#edit-and-save}

A page opens for reading. Click **Edit** at the top right to change it. The button appears only when you are allowed to edit the page.

Collabase saves your changes on its own about 1.5 seconds after you stop typing. Whiteboards save about 5 seconds after you stop drawing. The save button next to **Edit** shows the state:

| Save button | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Check mark | Everything is saved |
| **Unsaved** | A change is waiting to be saved. Click the button to save at once. |
| Spinner | The change is being saved |
| Warning icon, tooltip **Saving failed, try again** | The last save did not reach the server. Click the button to try again. |

If you leave the page while a change is still unsaved, the dialog **You have unsaved changes** opens. Click **Save & Continue** to keep your work, or **Discard Changes** to drop it.

<Note>
  With [live collaboration](/docs/collaboration) on, several people edit the same page at once and see each other's changes as they type. Without it, Collabase protects you from overwriting someone else: if another person saved the page while you were editing, the **Save conflict** dialog appears. **Overwrite anyway** replaces their changes. Their state stays available in the [version history](/docs/version-history).
</Note>

The toolbar of the editor has a mode switch with three positions: **Editing**, **Suggestion** and **Viewing**. **Suggestion** records your changes as proposals that the author accepts or rejects, see [Suggestions](/docs/suggestions).

## Rename a page and set its icon {#title-and-icon}

- **Title:** double-click the title in the bar at the top of the page, type the new title and press **Enter** or click elsewhere.
- **Icon:** click the icon in front of the title and pick an emoji. The icon also shows in the page tree.

You can also rename a page from the page tree, see [Organize pages](/docs/organize-pages#rename).

## Set the page status {#page-status}

Every page and whiteboard has a status that tells readers how far the content is. The status badge sits at the top right of the page. In edit mode, click it to choose another status.

| Status | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Work that is not ready. New pages and copies start here. |
| **In Progress** | Content that is being written |
| **In Review** | Content that waits for a review |
| **Published** | Content that is ready to be read |
| **Verified** | Content that someone checked and confirmed, marked with a shield |

An archived page shows the badge **Archived**. You archive a page with the **Archive** action, not with the status, see [Organize pages](/docs/organize-pages#archive).

<Note>
  Project guests never see pages in **Draft**. Members of the Space see a page in any status.
</Note>

## Page info {#page-info}

Click **Page info** (the **i** icon) at the top right of a page. The panel has four tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Details** | The author, **Created** and **Last changed**, and the custom fields of the page |
| **Labels** | The labels of the page. Type in **Search or create labels…** to add an existing label or create a new one. |
| **Tasks** | Work items linked to this page |
| **Versions** | Every saved version of the page, see [Version history](/docs/version-history) |

## Mark a page as favorite {#favorites}

Click the star at the top right of a page (**Add to favorites**). Your favorite pages appear under **Your Favorites** on your home page. Click the star again (**Remove from favorites**) to remove it.

## Duplicate a page {#duplicate}

Open the **...** menu at the top right of the page and click **Clone Page**, or right-click the page in the tree and click **Duplicate**. The copy appears next to the original with **(Copy)** after its title and the status **Draft**. Subpages are not copied.

## Whiteboards {#whiteboards}

A whiteboard is a drawing board for diagrams, wireframes and sketches. It comes with shape libraries for wireframes, forms, icons, data visualization, decision flows and DevOps diagrams.

- Click **Open in full screen** at the top right to draw in a separate browser tab.
- Several people can draw on the same whiteboard at once when [live collaboration](/docs/collaboration) is on.
- Whiteboards cannot be exported or translated. The **Export** entry is not shown in their **...** menu.
