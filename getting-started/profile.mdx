Your profile controls how others see you in Collabase and which notifications you receive. These settings only affect your own account.

Open your profile by clicking your **avatar** in the bottom-left corner of the sidebar and selecting **Profile** or **Settings**.

## Your details

On the **Profile** tab, set your full name, job title and avatar. Click **Save changes** when you are done.

If your company manages accounts through a directory (LDAP, SAML or SCIM), your name and email may be locked in Collabase. Change them in the directory instead.

## Language

Choose English, German, French, Italian or **Züritüütsch (KI-übersetzt)** on the **Preferences** tab. The interface reloads in the new language right away.

Züritüütsch is a Zurich German interface translated by AI. A text that has no Zurich German version yet appears in German, never in English. Emails and notifications for you follow the same rule. Texts that Collabase AI writes for you, such as meeting notes, stay in standard German. The first-run setup offers only English, German, French and Italian as the instance language.

## Notifications

New notifications show up under the **bell icon** in the top navigation bar. The number on the bell counts what you have not read yet and updates on its own. Open the bell to read, mark as read or unread, and archive. Entries about the same item are grouped, and **Mark all as read** clears the tab you are looking at.

You only get notifications about things you are allowed to open. If you lose access to a page or a project later, its older notifications stay in the list but show **No longer accessible** without their content.

### The inbox page

Select **Show all** at the bottom of the bell to open the inbox as a page. It has the tabs **All**, **Unread**, **Mentions** and **Archive**, and two filters: a category and a space. The space list holds only the spaces you can open. Your filters are remembered for you in this browser, and **Reset filters** clears them.

Tick entries to mark them as read or to archive them together. Archiving an entry that is part of a group archives the whole group. In the **Archive** tab, **Restore** brings an entry back to the inbox. Older entries load as you scroll.

With the keyboard, the arrow keys move between entries, **Enter** opens one, **E** archives it (in the archive, **E** restores it) and **U** marks it as read or unread.

### Choose what reaches you

Open your profile and select the **Notifications** tab. Each category has two switches: **In the app** and **Email**.

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Mentions and comments** | Someone @mentions you, comments on your work item, suggests a change on your page |
| **Assignments and work items** | A work item is assigned to you or changes |
| **Pages and shares** | A page is shared with you or a page you follow changes |
| **Projects** | Status reports, phase gates, budget warnings |
| **Time tracking** | Timesheets to review, your timesheet was reviewed |
| **Meetings** | Invitations, changes, cancellations, decisions on your topics |
| **Spaces and access** | You were added to a space, a guest accepted your invitation |
| **Studio and apps** | Messages from automations and apps |
| **Searches and filters** | Someone shares a [filter](/concepts/share-filters) with you, the emails of filters you [subscribed to](/concepts/subscribe-to-filters) |
| **Security** | Password changes, new devices, sign-in methods |

**Security** is always on, because those messages concern your account. Your administrator sets the defaults for everyone; a switch you change yourself overrides that default.

### Email frequency

Under **Email frequency**, choose **Right away** or **Once a day**. With **Once a day** you get one summary email at the time you pick, in your own time zone, with everything you have not read yet. Security emails always arrive right away.

If Collabase AI is available to you, **AI summary in the daily digest** starts the summary email with up to three sentences written by Collabase AI, marked as AI-generated. It is off until you switch it on. The titles of your notifications are then sent to the AI provider of your instance, and you can switch it off at any time. If the AI does not answer in ten seconds, the email goes out without the summary.

### Chat, browser and urgent items

If your administrator connected Slack or Microsoft Teams, **Chat** appears as a column. Link your account under **Chat** (Collabase finds it by your email address) and switch the categories you want as direct messages. Chat replaces email for those events, and security notices never go to chat. If the link stops working, you get email instead and see **Check the chat connection** here.

**Browser notifications** work per device. Turn them on for this device and allow them when your browser asks; **Browser** then appears as a column. Each device can be removed again.

**Remind me about urgent items** is on by default. If an urgent notification is still unread after 30 minutes, you get it once more by chat or email, even if you chose a daily summary. Details for administrators: [Chat, channels and browser notifications](/admin/chat-notifications).

### Decide in the inbox

Some entries can be decided without opening the object: a requested approval (**Approve** or **Reject**), a submitted timesheet if you review timesheets (**Approve**, or **Reject** with a reason), and a mention on a work item nobody is assigned to (**Take over**). Only the buttons you may use appear. Once the matter is decided, the entry shows how it ended, such as **Approved by you** or **Assigned to someone else**, and counts as read. Decisions exist only in the app, never in email or chat.

Every notification email has an **Unsubscribe from these emails** link at the bottom. It turns off email for that one category, and the summary email's link turns off all notification emails except security. You can switch them back on here at any time. Emails that were already sent cannot be recalled.

## Change your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Security tab">
    Click your avatar, then **Profile**, then **Security**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your current password">
    Collabase checks it before accepting a new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter and confirm the new password">
    It has to meet the password requirements your administrator has set. If it does not, Collabase tells you which ones are missing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    The new password applies right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a second sign-in step

With a second step, a stolen password is not enough to get into your account. After your password, Collabase asks for a six-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone. This is available if you sign in with a password.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Security tab">
    Click your avatar, then **Profile**, then **Security**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Set up">
    In the **Two-factor authentication** card, click **Set up** and enter your current password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    Open your authenticator app, scan the code, then enter the six-digit code the app shows and click **Confirm**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your backup codes">
    Collabase shows backup codes once. Copy them and keep them somewhere safe. Click **I have saved them**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each backup code signs you in once if you lose your phone. Use **New backup codes** in the same card to replace them; the old ones stop working. Click **Turn off** to remove the second step.

When you sign in, enter the code from the app. Tick **Trust this device for 30 days** to skip the code on that browser; it asks again after 30 days without a sign-in from it. A code works once. After too many wrong codes, the account is locked for a while.

## Sign in with a passkey

A passkey lets you sign in with your fingerprint, your face or your device PIN instead of a password. It replaces the password and the second step. You can add one for each device you use.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Security tab">
    Click your avatar, then **Profile**, then **Security**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the passkey">
    In the **Passkeys** card, type a name you will recognise, such as "Work laptop".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add passkey">
    Your browser asks you to confirm with your fingerprint, face or PIN. If you signed in more than a day ago, sign in again first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click the bin icon next to a passkey to remove it. Collabase does not let you remove the only way you can sign in. Set a password or link a company account first.
