Collabase Docs is the knowledge base of each Space. Every document is a page, and pages, whiteboards and folders sit in one page tree per Space.

## Create a page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Space">
    Pick the Space in the space switcher at the top of the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create, then Page">
    The page is created at the top level of the Space and opens in edit mode. You can also click **Docs** in the left rail and then **Create Page** at the top of the page tree.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it a title">
    Click the title in the bar at the top of the page and type. A page without a title shows as **Untitled** in the tree.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write">
    Collabase saves your changes on its own about 1.5 seconds after you stop typing. The save button next to **Edit** shows a check mark when everything is saved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To place the page under another page, drag it in the tree, see [Organize pages](/docs/organize-pages#reorder-and-nest). To start from a template instead of a blank page, see [Templates](/docs/templates).

## Use the editor

Type `/` anywhere on the page to insert a block: headings, lists, to-do lists, tables, code blocks, callouts, images, embeds, diagrams and macros. Type `@` to mention a person. See [Editor elements](/docs/editor-elements) for the full list.

## Restore an earlier version

Collabase saves a version with every save of the page content.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the versions">
    Click **Page info** (the **i** icon) at the top right of the page, then the **Versions** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a version">
    Click a version to preview it. Number, author and time are shown next to each entry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restore it">
    Click **Restore this version** in the preview, then **Restore** in the dialog **Restore this version?**. The message **Version restored successfully** confirms it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The restore replaces the current content for everyone. Details are on [Version history](/docs/version-history#restore).
