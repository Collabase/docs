## Your first steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open your Collabase URL and sign in. See [Sign in](/getting-started/sign-in).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open or create a Space">
    Pick a Space from the left sidebar, or create a new one. See [Spaces](/getting-started/spaces).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your team">
    Invite colleagues to the Space and give each one a role. See [Invite members](/getting-started/invite-members).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a project and tasks">
    Create a project and add the first tasks. See [Projects](/getting-started/projects) and [Tasks](/getting-started/tasks).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan the work">
    Move tasks across the board and plan them from the backlog. See [Board and backlog](/getting-started/boards).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write things down">
    Create your first page in Collabase Docs. See [Pages in Collabase Docs](/getting-started/docs-pages).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  This guide is for people who work in Collabase every day. To install and run Collabase, start with [Installation](/installation) and [First login](/first-run).
</Note>
