Customers are people outside your organization who send requests through a service portal. They sign in to the portal only. They never see projects, pages or colleagues, and they do not count as users of your installation.

Administrators manage customers under **Administration → Customers**. The page has two tabs, **Customers** and **Organizations**.

## Organizations

An organization groups the customers of one company. Customers of an organization see the requests of their colleagues if the portal allows it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the organization">
    Open the **Organizations** tab and select **Add organization**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the name and the mail domains">
    Type the name. Add the mail domains of the company, one per line, for example `example.ch`. A domain belongs to exactly one organization.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change a name or its domains, open the menu of the row and select **Edit**. Deleting an organization leaves its customers in place, without an organization.

## Invite a customer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invitation">
    Open the **Customers** tab and select **Invite customer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Enter the email address and the name. Pick an organization if the customer belongs to one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Save. Collabase sends the customer a link to set a password. The link works once and expires after a few days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  An address of a colleague or a guest cannot be used for a customer. Collabase does not say which of the two it is.
</Note>

The customer opens the link, chooses a password and lands in the portal. The page speaks of the portal, not of a project.

## Lock a customer

Locking stops a customer from signing in. Their requests and comments stay. Open the menu of the row and select **Lock**; **Unlock** reverses it. Only system administrators can lock customers.

## Anonymize a customer

Anonymizing removes the personal data of a customer for good: name, address and sign-in. Requests and comments stay, without a name. Collabase asks for confirmation first.

<Warning>
  Anonymizing cannot be undone. Only system administrators can do it.
</Warning>

## Who sees what

- System administrators manage all customers and organizations.
- Administrators of a service project add customers to their own portal and see the customers of their projects.
- Customers who were switched to a different kind of account cannot be changed into users or administrators. Create a separate account for a colleague instead.

<Info>
  Free fields in request forms may hold personal data. Tell your customers what they should not enter.
</Info>
