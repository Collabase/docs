The risk register lists what could go wrong in the projects of a Space, rated by probability and impact. Every risk sits on a 3 by 3 matrix, so the critical ones stand out. Each risk can name an owner, a project and the work item that handles it.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

Risks are [Registry](/registry/overview) Objects. The register reads them from one Object Type and maps its fields to the roles it needs:

| Role | Required | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Probability** | Yes | 1 to 3 |
| **Impact** | Yes | 1 to 3 |
| **Score** | No | Probability times impact |
| **Status** | No | The state of the risk |
| **Owner** | No | Who watches the risk |
| **Review date** | No | When to look at it again |
| **Project** | No | The project the risk belongs to |
| **Measure** | No | The work item that handles the risk |

## Set up the register {#set-up}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch on the module">
    In **Space Settings → Features**, switch on **Risks**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a schema">
    Open the Space's **Risks** page. Choose **Create from template**, which creates the Project Governance schema with risks, assumptions and decisions. Or accept the **Suggested connection** with **Connect**, or use **Map fields yourself** for a schema you already have.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show risks in projects">
    In each project, switch on **Risks** under **Settings → Project features**. The project gets a **Risks** tab with its own risks. If the risk type has no project field yet, use **Add project field**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read the matrix {#matrix}

| Score | Band |
|---|---|
| 1 to 2 | **Low** |
| 3 to 4 | **Medium** |
| 5 to 9 | **High**, counted as critical |

Risks without a rating are counted as **… unrated**. Filter the matrix with **All projects** or one project, and with **Critical only**. To re-rate a risk, drag it to another cell.

## Record a risk {#record}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    Click **Record risk**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe and rate it">
    Enter the **Title** ("What could go wrong?"), choose probability and impact, and optionally the owner, the project and the measure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link the measure">
    For the measure, search for an existing work item and pick it. The risk then shows **Measure** with the work item key. Create the work item first if it does not exist yet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Recording a risk needs contributor rights on the risk schema in the Registry and the right to edit work items in the Space.

## Troubleshooting {#troubleshooting}

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **No risk schema connected yet** | A Space manager connects a schema, see [Set up the register](#set-up) |
| **Recording risks needs contributor rights on the risk schema in the registry.** | Ask the schema's owner for contributor rights |
| **Risks are not tied to projects yet** | Use **Add project field**. It needs the editor right on the schema |
