The **Backlog** lists the open work items of a project and the cycles they are planned into. Rank the work by dragging it, then drag it into the next [cycle](/projects/sprints). It is where sprint planning happens.

## What the Backlog shows

| Area | Content |
|---|---|
| **Active Cycle** banner | The running cycle and its **Progress**, when one is active |
| One lane per cycle | Every planned and active cycle, collapsible, with its status and an actions menu |
| Backlog section | Open work items in no cycle, with the count, for example **12 work items** |

Completed work items do not appear in the backlog section. When the project limits cycles to certain types under **Settings → Cycles → Allowed work item types**, only those types are listed. Work items without a type always appear.

## Rank the work {#rank}

Drag a work item up or down within the backlog or within a cycle lane. The new order is saved at once and everyone sees it. Put the most important work at the top.

You can also edit the estimate of a work item directly in its row, using the project's [estimation method](/projects/sprints#estimation).

## Plan work into a cycle {#plan-into-a-cycle}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a cycle if needed">
    Click **Create Cycle**, enter a **Name** and optionally a **Cycle Goal**, **Start Date** and **Due Date**, then confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move the work items">
    Drag a work item from the backlog into the cycle lane. Or right-click it and choose **Move to Cycle**, then the cycle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the lane">
    The work item appears in the cycle lane. To take it out again, right-click it and choose **Move to Backlog**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Work items move one at a time. When the project has **Carry child work items** or **Carry parent work item** switched on, the sub-items or the parent move along (see [Subtasks and cycles](/projects/sprints#subtasks-and-sprints)).

<Note>
  Moving work between the backlog and cycles needs the **Manage cycle scope** permission. Without it, the lanes are locked and a drop shows **Move blocked**. Creating a cycle needs **Create cycle**.
</Note>

**Add work item** below the backlog opens the create dialog.

## Limits

- The Backlog has no search and no quick filters. To find work items by field, use the [Work Items table](/projects/list-view) or [search](/concepts/search).
- There is no multi-select. You move, rank and edit work items one at a time.
