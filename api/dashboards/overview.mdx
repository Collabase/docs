The Dashboards API gives integrations the free dashboards of the person who issued the token: the ones they own and the ones shared with them. Every call runs as that person, with their rights, and every gadget computes with those rights.

## Scopes {#scopes}

| Scope | Allows |
|---|---|
| `dashboards:read` | [List](/api/dashboards/list-dashboards) and [get](/api/dashboards/get-dashboard) dashboards and read [the data of a gadget](/api/dashboards/gadget-data) |
| `dashboards:write` | [Create](/api/dashboards/create-dashboard), [change](/api/dashboards/update-dashboard), [delete](/api/dashboards/delete-dashboard) and [share](/api/dashboards/replace-dashboard-shares); includes `dashboards:read` |

The data of a gadget also needs the scope of what the gadget shows, for example `tasks:read` for a chart of work items, `finance:read` for a cost gadget or `meetings:read` for upcoming meetings. Without it the answer is `403 INSUFFICIENT_SCOPE` and names the scope. Existing tokens do not get the new scopes automatically; issue a new token under **Profile → Security**.

## Conventions {#conventions}

- **Only free dashboards.** Homes (your own included), the default home, project homes and public links are not part of the API. Their IDs answer `404 NOT_FOUND`.
- **A dashboard you may not read does not exist.** Its ID answers `404 NOT_FOUND`, exactly like an ID that does not exist.
- **Who may do what.** The owner and people with the right to edit change a dashboard. Only the owner shares and deletes it. A guest can only be given the right to view. See [Share a dashboard](/concepts/share-dashboards).
- **Changes name their version.** `PATCH` carries the `updatedAt` you read as `baseUpdatedAt`. A dashboard changed since answers `409 CONFLICT`.
- **Creating is not idempotent.** Two `POST` calls create two dashboards.
- **Pages.** Lists are paged with a cursor. `limit` defaults to 50 and is cut down to 100.
- **Strict bodies.** Unknown fields are rejected with `422 VALIDATION_ERROR`.
