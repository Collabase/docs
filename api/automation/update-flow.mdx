---
title: "Rename or delete a flow"
description: "Rename a flow or delete it with its runs through the public API."
api: "PATCH /api/v1/automation/flows/{flowId}"
---

`PATCH` renames the flow. `DELETE` removes the flow with its versions and runs.

<Info>
  Scope `automation:write`. The token owner needs the editor role.
</Info>

## Body of PATCH

<ParamField body="name" type="string" required>
  The new name.
</ParamField>
