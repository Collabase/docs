---
title: "Switch a flow on or off"
description: "Switch a published flow on or off; a sensitive workspace asks for approval before a flow is switched on."
api: "POST /api/v1/automation/flows/{flowId}/status"
---

<Info>
  Scope `automation:write`.
</Info>

## Body

<ParamField body="enabled" type="boolean" required>
  `true` switches the flow on, `false` off. Only a published flow can be switched on (`422 FLOW_NOT_PUBLISHED`). In a sensitive workspace switching on answers `409 APPROVAL_REQUESTED` after asking the admins.
</ParamField>
