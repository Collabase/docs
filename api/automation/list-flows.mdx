---
title: "List flows"
description: "Page through the flows of one automation workspace, filtered by status or name."
api: "GET /api/v1/automation/flows"
---

Returns one page of the flows of a workspace.

<Info>
  Scope `automation:read`. The token owner needs any role in the workspace.
</Info>

## Query parameters

<ParamField query="workspaceId" type="string" required>
  The workspace.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="status" type="string">
  `ENABLED` or `DISABLED`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="q" type="string">
  Part of the flow name.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="cursor" type="string">
  The `nextCursor` of the previous page.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="limit" type="integer" default="50">
  Flows per page, at most 100.
</ParamField>
