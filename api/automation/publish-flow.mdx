---
title: "Publish a flow"
description: "Publish the draft of a flow, or ask the admins of a sensitive Space to approve it."
api: "POST /api/v1/automation/flows/{flowId}/publish"
---

Publishes the draft. A draft with open issues answers `422`.

<Info>
  Scope `automation:write`.
</Info>

In a sensitive Space the call does not publish. It asks the admins and answers `{ "status": "REQUESTED", "requestId": "..." }`.
