---
title: "Get agenda"
description: "Read a meeting's agenda sections and items in order, with their outcomes, links and the token owner's rights."
api: "GET /api/v1/meetings/{meetingId}/agenda"
---

An agenda holds items, optionally grouped into sections. Organizers edit it, and so do participants
when the meeting allows it. Each section and item carries its `updatedAt`, which a later change sends
back as `baseUpdatedAt`.

<Info>
  Scope `meetings:read`.
</Info>

## Path parameters

<ParamField path="meetingId" type="string" required>The meeting.</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="data" type="object" required>
  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ResponseField name="sections" type="object[]" required>Sections in order, with `id`, `title`, `order` and `updatedAt`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="items" type="object[]" required>
      Items of every section and those without one, each group in order. Each item has `id`,
      `sectionId`, `title`, `description`, `ownerId`, `ownerName`, `estimateMinutes`, `notes`,
      `state` (`ACCEPTED`, `OPEN` or `DONE`), `order`, `updatedAt`, `decisionCount` and `carryCount`
      (how often the topic was handed on from one meeting to the next).
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="canEdit" type="boolean" required>The token owner may change the agenda now.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="participantsMayEdit" type="boolean" required>Participants edit the agenda too, not only organizers.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="outcomes" type="object[]" required>The outcomes of every item, as in [List outcomes](/api/meetings/outcomes/list-outcomes).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="canRecordOutcomes" type="boolean" required>The token owner may record outcomes now; only while the meeting runs.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="links" type="object[]" required>Work items and pages linked to the items.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="proposals" type="object[]" required>Submitted topics waiting for review: all of them for organizers, the token owner's own for everyone else.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="topics" type="object" required>Topic submission settings: `submissionsToAgenda`, `submissionLeadHours`, `submissionDeadline`, `submissionTarget`, `canSubmit`, `hasBacklog`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="canChangeSetting" type="boolean" required>The token owner may switch `participantsMayEdit`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="canReview" type="boolean" required>The token owner may accept, park or reject proposals.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="linkSpaceId" type="string | null" required>The meeting's Space.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="canCreatePage" type="boolean" required>The token owner may create pages from items in the meeting's Space.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<RequestExample>

```bash cURL
curl -X GET "https://your-collabase.ch/api/v1/meetings/cm2meet0001abcd/agenda" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer clb_xYz123ABCD..."
```
