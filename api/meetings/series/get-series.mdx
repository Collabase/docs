---
title: "Get series"
description: "Read a meeting series with its rule and one page of its occurrences, oldest first."
api: "GET /api/v1/meetings/series/{seriesId}"
---

Returns the series and one page of 50 occurrences, oldest first. Without `page`, you get the page
holding the next occurrence. Whoever may see the series' latest occurrence sees the list of dates
and states; each occurrence still checks its own access when you open it, so in a private series a
participant who joined later can open occurrences from their first one on.

<Info>
  Scope `meetings:read`.
</Info>

## Path parameters

<ParamField path="seriesId" type="string" required>The series, as `series.id` from [Get meeting](/api/meetings/get-meeting).</ParamField>

## Query parameters

<ParamField query="page" type="number">Page of occurrences, from 1. A page beyond the last returns the last page. Other query parameters are rejected with `422`.</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="data" type="object" required>
  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ResponseField name="id" type="string" required />
    <ResponseField name="title" type="string" required />
    <ResponseField name="rrule" type="string" required>The current rule.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="seriesTimezone" type="string" required>The time zone the series keeps its wall-clock time in.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="timezone" type="string" required>The token owner's time zone.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="instances" type="object[]" required>Occurrences with `id`, `startsAt`, `status` and `isMoved` (held at another time than the rule says).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="page" type="number" required />
    <ResponseField name="pageCount" type="number" required />
    <ResponseField name="manageMeetingId" type="string | null" required>The occurrence to send a [rule change](/api/meetings/series/change-series-rule) through, when the token owner may change the rule.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="nextStart" type="object | null" required>`date` and `time` of the next occurrence not held yet, in the series' time zone.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<RequestExample>

```bash cURL
curl -X GET "https://your-collabase.ch/api/v1/meetings/series/cm9seri0001?page=1" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer clb_xYz123ABCD..."
```

</RequestExample>
