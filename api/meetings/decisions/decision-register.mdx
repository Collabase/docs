---
title: "Search decision register"
description: "Search decisions across every meeting the token owner may read, newest first, filtered and paginated."
api: "GET /api/v1/meetings/decisions"
---

Returns decisions across every meeting the token owner may read, newest first. Decisions of private
meetings appear only for their participants.

<Info>
  Scope `meetings:read`.
</Info>

## Query parameters

<ParamField query="q" type="string">Case-insensitive search in titles, up to 200 characters.</ParamField>
<ParamField query="spaceId" type="string">Only decisions of meetings in this Space.</ParamField>
<ParamField query="projectId" type="string">Only decisions of meetings in this project.</ParamField>
<ParamField query="seriesId" type="string">Only decisions of this series.</ParamField>
<ParamField query="personId" type="string">Only decisions this member took part in.</ParamField>
<ParamField query="status" type="string">`VALID`, `SUPERSEDED` or `REVOKED`.</ParamField>
<ParamField query="from" type="string">`YYYY-MM-DD`, inclusive, in the token owner's time zone.</ParamField>
<ParamField query="to" type="string">`YYYY-MM-DD`, inclusive, in the token owner's time zone.</ParamField>
<ParamField query="page" type="number" default="1">Page number.</ParamField>
<ParamField query="pageSize" type="number" default="25">Entries per page. Values above 100 are treated as 100.</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="data" type="object" required>
  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ResponseField name="entries" type="object[]" required>
      Decisions with the fields of [List meeting decisions](/api/meetings/decisions/list-decisions),
      plus `meetingTitle`, `meetingStartsAt` and `space` (`id`, `name`) of the meeting they were
      taken in.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="pagination" type="object" required>`page`, `pageSize`, `total` and `totalPages`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="series" type="object[]" required>Series you can filter by: those with at least one decision the token owner may read, with `id` and `title`.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>
