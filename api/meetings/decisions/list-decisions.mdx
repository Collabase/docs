---
title: "List meeting decisions"
description: "Read the decisions taken in one meeting, oldest first, and whether the token owner may record more right now."
api: "GET /api/v1/meetings/{meetingId}/decisions"
---

A decision is recorded in a running meeting and stays `VALID` until it is replaced (`SUPERSEDED`) or
revoked (`REVOKED`). A replaced decision points at its successor, so the register shows the whole
chain. This endpoint returns the decisions of one meeting in the order they were taken; to search
across meetings, use the [decision register](/api/meetings/decisions/decision-register).

<Info>
  Scope `meetings:read`.
</Info>

## Path parameters

<ParamField path="meetingId" type="string" required>The meeting.</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="data" type="object" required>
  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ResponseField name="decisions" type="object[]" required>
      <Expandable title="decision">
    <ResponseField name="id" type="string" required />
    <ResponseField name="meetingId" type="string" required />
    <ResponseField name="title" type="string" required />
    <ResponseField name="description" type="string | null" required />
    <ResponseField name="rationale" type="string | null" required />
    <ResponseField name="status" type="string" required>`VALID`, `SUPERSEDED` or `REVOKED`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="agendaItemId" type="string | null" required />
    <ResponseField name="persons" type="object[]" required>Decision makers with `userId` and `name`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="decidedAt" type="string" required>ISO 8601 in UTC.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="supersededById" type="string | null" required>The newer decision that replaced this one.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="supersedesId" type="string | null" required>The older decision this one replaced.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="pollId" type="string | null" required>The poll whose result it records.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="updatedAt" type="string" required>Send it as `baseUpdatedAt` when you reword the decision.</ResponseField>
      </Expandable>
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="canRecord" type="boolean" required>The token owner may record, reword, replace and revoke decisions in this meeting right now; only while it runs.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>
