---
title: "List participants"
description: "Read who is invited to a meeting, with role and invitation answer, plus answer counts and the token owner's rights."
api: "GET /api/v1/meetings/{meetingId}/participants"
---

A meeting has exactly one organizer. Co-organizers have the same rights except transferring the
organizer role. Participants and optional participants receive the invitation; what they may edit
depends on the meeting's `participantsMayEdit` setting.

| Role | Assignable through the API |
|---|---|
| **`ORGANIZER`** | No; moves only by transfer in the app |
| **`CO_ORGANIZER`** | Members only |
| **`PARTICIPANT`** | Members and guests |
| **`OPTIONAL`** | Members and guests |

Members are listed by name; only external guests show their `email`.

<Info>
  Scope `meetings:read`.
</Info>

## Path parameters

<ParamField path="meetingId" type="string" required>The meeting.</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="data" type="object" required>
  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ResponseField name="participants" type="object[]" required>
      Every participant with `id`, `userId` (`null` for a guest), `name`, `image`, `email` (guests
      only), `role`, `rsvp` and `isSelf`. `rsvp` is `NEEDS_ACTION`, `ACCEPTED`, `TENTATIVE` or
      `DECLINED`.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="counts" type="object" required>Number of participants per `rsvp` value.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="canManage" type="boolean" required>The token owner may invite, remove and change participants.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="canTransferOrganizer" type="boolean" required>The token owner may hand the organizer role on, in the app.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="ownRsvp" type="string | null" required>The token owner's own answer; `null` when they are not invited.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="canRespond" type="boolean" required>The token owner is invited and the meeting is still open.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<RequestExample>

```bash cURL

  -H "Authorization: Bearer clb_xYz123ABCD..."
```
