---
title: "List outcomes"
description: "Read every outcome of a meeting with its type, text, responsible person, due date and linked work item."
api: "GET /api/v1/meetings/{meetingId}/outcomes"
---

Returns every outcome of the meeting, grouped by agenda item and in their order within each item.
Outcomes belong to an agenda item and are recorded while the meeting runs.

<Info>
  Scope `meetings:read`.
</Info>

## Path parameters

<ParamField path="meetingId" type="string" required>The meeting.</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="data" type="object[]" required>
  <Expandable title="outcome">
    <ResponseField name="id" type="string" required />
    <ResponseField name="agendaItemId" type="string" required>The agenda item it was recorded under.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="type" type="string" required>`INFORMATION` or `TASK`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="text" type="string" required />
    <ResponseField name="responsibleId" type="string | null" required />
    <ResponseField name="responsibleName" type="string | null" required />
    <ResponseField name="dueDate" type="string | null" required>`YYYY-MM-DD`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="task" type="object | null" required>The work item made from it: `{ "visible": true, "key", "title", "href" }`, or `{ "visible": false }` when the token owner has no access to its project.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="updatedAt" type="string" required>Send it as `baseUpdatedAt` when you change the outcome.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<RequestExample>

```bash cURL
curl -X GET "https://your-collabase.ch/api/v1/meetings/cm2meet0001abcd/outcomes" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer clb_xYz123ABCD..."
```

</RequestExample>
