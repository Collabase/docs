A meeting has exactly one organizer. Co-organizers have the same rights except transferring the
organizer role. Participants and optional participants receive the invitation; what they may edit
depends on the meeting's `participantsMayEdit` setting.

| Role | Assignable through the API |
|---|---|
| `ORGANIZER` | No; moves only by transfer in the app |
| `CO_ORGANIZER` | Members only |
| `PARTICIPANT` | Members and guests |
| `OPTIONAL` | Members and guests |

## List participants

`GET /api/v1/meetings/{meetingId}/participants` · scope `meetings:read`

Returns every participant with `role` and answer (`rsvp`: `NEEDS_ACTION`, `ACCEPTED`, `TENTATIVE`,
`DECLINED`). Members are listed by name; only external guests show their `email`.

## Invite someone

`POST /api/v1/meetings/{meetingId}/participants` · scope `meetings:write` · `201`

Send either a member or an external guest:

<ParamField body="userId" type="string">A member's id. Use instead of `email`.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="email" type="string">An external guest's address. Guests cannot sign in and never co-organize.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="role" type="string" default="PARTICIPANT">`CO_ORGANIZER` (members only), `PARTICIPANT` or `OPTIONAL`.</ParamField>

In a planned meeting the new participant receives the invitation shortly after. A meeting holds up
to 500 participants.

## Change or remove a participant

`PATCH /api/v1/meetings/{meetingId}/participants/{participantId}` · scope `meetings:write`

<ParamField body="role" type="string">`CO_ORGANIZER`, `PARTICIPANT` or `OPTIONAL`.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="rsvp" type="string">`NEEDS_ACTION`, `ACCEPTED`, `TENTATIVE` or `DECLINED`, recorded on the participant's behalf.</ParamField>

Send at least one of the two.

`DELETE /api/v1/meetings/{meetingId}/participants/{participantId}` · scope `meetings:write`

Removes the participant; in a planned meeting they receive a cancellation shortly after. The
organizer cannot be removed (`409 CONFLICT`); the organizer role cannot be changed either.

## Answer an invitation

`POST /api/v1/meetings/{meetingId}/rsvp` · scope `meetings:write`

Records the token owner's own answer.

<ParamField body="rsvp" type="string" required>`ACCEPTED`, `TENTATIVE` or `DECLINED`.</ParamField>
