title: "Meeting series API"
sidebarTitle: "Series"
description: "Turn a meeting into a recurring series through the API, change or end its rule, and read its occurrences page by page."
---

A series is created from a meeting, which becomes its first occurrence. Later occurrences are created
as they come up and copy the series' participants and agenda template. The rule is an iCalendar
`RRULE` and keeps its wall-clock time in the time zone the series was planned in.

## Create a series

`POST /api/v1/meetings/{meetingId}/series` · scope `meetings:write` · `201`

<ParamField body="rrule" type="string" required>
  For example `FREQ=WEEKLY;BYDAY=TH` or `FREQ=MONTHLY;BYDAY=1MO;COUNT=10`. Up to 200 characters.
</ParamField>

The meeting must be a draft or planned one-off meeting. Returns the id of the new series.

## Change or end the rule

`PATCH /api/v1/meetings/{meetingId}/series` · scope `meetings:write`

Send it for any occurrence of the series.

<ParamField body="rrule" type="string" required>The new rule. To end the series, send the current rule with `UNTIL`.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="startDate" type="string" required>`YYYY-MM-DD` from which the new rule applies. Must be after the last meeting held.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="dryRun" type="boolean" required>`true` only reports the effect without changing anything.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="dropped" type="object[]" required>
  Planned occurrences the new rule no longer has, with `id`, `startsAt` and the number of open topics.
  They are cancelled and their open topics move to the series backlog.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="next" type="string[]" required>
  The next dates under the new rule.
</ResponseField>

Send the change with `dryRun: true` first, show the result, then send it again with `dryRun: false`.
Occurrences already held never change.

## Read a series

`GET /api/v1/meetings/series/{seriesId}` · scope `meetings:read`

<ParamField query="page" type="number">
  Page of occurrences, 50 per page, oldest first. Without it, the page holding the next occurrence.
</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="instances" type="object[]" required>
  Occurrences with `id`, `startsAt`, `status` and `isMoved` (held at another time than the rule says).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="rrule" type="string" required>The current rule.</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="manageMeetingId" type="string | null" required>
  The occurrence to send a rule change through, when the token owner may change the rule.
</ResponseField>

Each occurrence checks its own access when you open it: in a private series, a participant who joined
later sees occurrences from their first one on.
