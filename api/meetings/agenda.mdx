An agenda holds items, optionally grouped into sections. Organizers edit it, and so do participants
when the meeting allows it. Outcomes belong to an agenda item and are recorded while the meeting runs.

## Read the agenda

`GET /api/v1/meetings/{meetingId}/agenda` · scope `meetings:read`

Returns `sections` and `items` in order. Each item carries its `updatedAt`, which a later change sends
back as `baseUpdatedAt`. `canEdit` says whether the token owner may change the agenda.

## Sections

| Method and path | Body |
|---|---|
| `POST /api/v1/meetings/{meetingId}/agenda/sections` → `201` | `{ "title": "…" }` |
| `PATCH /api/v1/meetings/{meetingId}/agenda/sections/{sectionId}` | `{ "title": "…", "baseUpdatedAt": "…" }` |
| `DELETE /api/v1/meetings/{meetingId}/agenda/sections/{sectionId}` | none |
| `POST /api/v1/meetings/{meetingId}/agenda/sections/{sectionId}/move` | `{ "afterSectionId": "…" }`, or `null` to place it first |

## Items

`POST /api/v1/meetings/{meetingId}/agenda/items` · `201`

<ParamField body="title" type="string" required>Up to 200 characters.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="sectionId" type="string | null" default="null">Section to add it to; `null` for the agenda without section.</ParamField>

`PATCH /api/v1/meetings/{meetingId}/agenda/items/{itemId}`

<ParamField body="baseUpdatedAt" type="string" required>The item's `updatedAt` as you read it.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="title" type="string">Up to 200 characters.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="description" type="string | null">Up to 5,000 characters.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="ownerId" type="string | null">The member who presents the item.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="estimateMinutes" type="number | null">Planned time, 1 to 720 minutes.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="notes" type="object | null">Rich-text notes as `{ "version": 1, "content": [ … ] }` in the editor's document format, up to 200 KB.</ParamField>

Send `baseUpdatedAt` and at least one other field.

`DELETE /api/v1/meetings/{meetingId}/agenda/items/{itemId}`

`POST /api/v1/meetings/{meetingId}/agenda/items/{itemId}/move`

<ParamField body="sectionId" type="string | null" required>Target section, or `null` for the agenda without section.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="afterItemId" type="string | null" required>The item it follows, or `null` to place it first.</ParamField>

All item and section changes need scope `meetings:write`.

## Outcomes

`GET /api/v1/meetings/{meetingId}/outcomes` · scope `meetings:read`

Every outcome of the meeting with its type, text, responsible person, due date and linked work item.

`POST /api/v1/meetings/{meetingId}/agenda/items/{itemId}/outcomes` · scope `meetings:write` · `201`

<ParamField body="type" type="string" required>`INFORMATION` or `TASK`.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="text" type="string" required>Up to 2,000 characters.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="responsibleId" type="string | null" default="null">The member responsible.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="dueDate" type="string | null" default="null">`YYYY-MM-DD`.</ParamField>

`PATCH /api/v1/meetings/{meetingId}/outcomes/{outcomeId}` · scope `meetings:write`

Takes `text`, `responsibleId` and `dueDate`, plus the required `baseUpdatedAt`.

`DELETE /api/v1/meetings/{meetingId}/outcomes/{outcomeId}` · scope `meetings:write`

Outcomes are recorded only while the meeting runs; otherwise the call returns
`409 MEETING_NOT_RUNNING`.
