A blog post is a page of type **Blog** in a Space. The Intranet shows the published blog posts of one Space, the **News Space**, in its **News** section.

<Warning>
  Collabase has no way to create a blog post in the app at the moment. The **Create** menu offers **Page**, **Whiteboard** and **Folder** only, and no screen links to a Space's blog. Blog posts that already exist are still shown. Until creating posts is possible again, use a page in a public Space for announcements.
</Warning>

## Where blog posts appear {#where}

| Place | What it shows |
|---|---|
| The **News** section of the Intranet | Published blog posts of the News Space, newest first, up to 20 |
| The address `/{space-slug}/blog` | The blog posts of that Space, up to 50. No menu entry leads there. |

An administrator picks the **News Space** in the Intranet settings, see [Intranet](/intranet/overview).
